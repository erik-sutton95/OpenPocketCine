New and changed

- Motion Control can loop a programmed move with linear zoom.
- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) offers lens presets and a custom 1.00–2.00× factor for live view, videos and photos.
- Report a problem is available before your first camera connection, with optional technical details and screenshots.

Fixes

- Improved recovery when an established preview loses its video format.
- Pairing no longer times out on phones where the camera approval never appeared, and opening the camera's own gallery opens Media here.
- Preview no longer drops while Media is open during a repair, stalled video recovers in about 2 seconds, and a dropped decoder frame now triggers repair instead of a smeared picture.

What to test

- Try a short looping move with zoom; check start, pause, resume and stop with clearance around the gimbal.
- Toggle DE-SQ and try presets, Custom, LUTs and playback. Check the custom factor survives a restart and shared files remain unchanged.
- Open Report a problem before pairing. Then connect three times and check picture recovery after Media, Settings and background use.

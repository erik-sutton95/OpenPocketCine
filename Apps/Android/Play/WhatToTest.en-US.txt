New and changed

- Scopes, paused playback and camera status updates use less power. Clips without a proxy are graded at monitoring size.
- Osmo Action 6 support: live view, Normal 10-bit and D-Log M color, the official D-Log M LUT, and an APERTURE control in place of FOCUS. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.
- Experimental Multiview is now on Android: tap the grid button on Your cameras and add up to four Pocket or Nano cameras.

Fixes

- Deleting a clip from clip playback works again: Delete and Cancel no longer play or pause the clip behind the dialog.
- Live video no longer stops shortly after connecting while the app is busy.

What to test

- Pause a clip with a LUT on, then seek and change the look: the picture should update right away.
- If you have an Action 6: pair it, confirm live view appears, then try APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.
- In Media, open a clip, tap the trash icon, and try Cancel, then Delete.

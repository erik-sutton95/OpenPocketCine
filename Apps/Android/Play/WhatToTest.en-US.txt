New and changed

- Osmo Action 6 support: live view, Normal 10-bit and D-Log M color, and the official D-Log M LUT. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.
- On Action 6, an APERTURE control takes the place of FOCUS. It shows the live f-number and sets Auto, f/2.6, f/2.8, Starburst f/4 or SuperNight f/2.0.
- Experimental Multiview is now on Android: tap the grid button on Your cameras and add up to four Pocket or Nano cameras.

Fixes

- Live video no longer stops shortly after connecting while the app is busy.

What to test

- If you have an Action 6: pair it, confirm live view appears, then try APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.
- Add two or more cameras in Multiview, then close it and check each camera returns to its own Wi-Fi.

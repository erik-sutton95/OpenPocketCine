New features

- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt gauges from the camera's own sensor. Roll the handle in FPV and check the roll gauge moves the right way.
- Point the lens straight down: a round bubble replaces the gauges. Check it centers when the shot is truly top-down.
- Gimbal drawer → Double-tap → Level. Angle the handle forward, double-tap the joystick, and check the toast says Leveled top-down (or how far off it stopped).
- In Multiview, try Record all and Live View and back.

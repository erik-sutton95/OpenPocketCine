New and changed

- View Assist EV toggles a slim white line and sun marker with +3/−3 ends. It reads the camera directly and moves clear of the toolbar in DISP 1.
- Motion Control loops through saved zooms and offers Restart while paused. Zoom controls stay accessible; D-Log2 blocks zoom-changing programs.
- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) offers lens presets and a custom 1.00–2.00× factor for live view, videos and photos.

Fixes

- EV is separate from compensation settings. It shows a dash when camera metering is unavailable; DISP 2 and playback hide it.
- Auto exposure shows the camera's applied shutter under EV. Missing or unsupported shutter readings leave only EV visible.
- Pairing no longer times out on phones where the camera approval never appeared.
- Stalled video recovers in about 2 seconds, and opening the camera's own gallery opens Media here.

What to test

- Toggle EV, rotate and expand/collapse the toolbar; check that it stays clear of the meter. Compare EV and Auto shutter with the camera as lighting changes, then switch to Manual and back.
- Save A/B/C at different zooms; try both loop directions, Pause/Resume and Restart. Drag the editor, change a duration, then close/reopen it and check saved settings remain.
- Hold DE-SQ for presets, Custom and direction. Check framing with LUTs and guides in live view, videos and photos.

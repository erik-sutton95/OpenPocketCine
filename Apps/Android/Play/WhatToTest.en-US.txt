New and changed

- Experimental Multiview is now on Android: tap the grid button on Your cameras and add up to four Pocket or Nano cameras.
- Each tile shows the live picture, settings and timecode, with its own LUT and record button. Double-tap a tile for full Live View.

Fixes

- In Multiview, a Pocket that ignores its Wi-Fi identity check right after pairing gets one wake and a retry.

What to test

- Set up Phone hotspot (Mobile Hotspot, WPA2) or Local Wi-Fi, then add two or more cameras. Approve pairing on a camera if asked.
- Try Record all, each tile's record button, Grid/Center stage, FIT/FILL, DISP, LUTs and Live View and back.
- Close Multiview and check each camera returns to its own Wi-Fi.

New and changed

- UI 2.0: Sora/Lucide, consistent buttons and signal colors. Media footer controls, pull-refresh, and hold/sideways selection with native vertical scrolling.
- Pocket 4 Pro zoom: tap 1×/3×, double tap 6×/12×, hold for the larger lens dial extending to the screen edge above camera controls.
- New scopes, LIGHTS and ND start centered until placed. AUDIO moves, supports vertical/horizontal bars and optional channel dB readings.

Fixes

- Pocket 3 FORMAT lists 16:9 and 1:1 without hanging on 9:16. Vertical recording stays on body Lock Portrait unless the camera already reports 9:16.
- Pocket 3 shutter Speed is not stuck at 1/60; Angle choices change exposure.
- 16:9 frame guides on 1:1 live stay on the square picture, not the side bars.
- Crash hardening: sharing a photo no longer closes the app, a Bluetooth drop recovers instead of crashing, and face auto-focus no longer aborts under load.

What to test

- On Pocket 3, switch FORMAT 16:9 and 1:1; confirm 9:16 does not hang. Change shutter Speed and Angle. In 1:1, turn on a 16:9 guide and check it sits on the picture.
- Check blur over bright/dark footage. Rotate pages; tap versus hold drums. Try assist tabs, audio, zoom, lock, Media and TalkBack.
- On Pocket 4 Pro, compare still shots and slow pans with LUT/scopes off and on. Share diagnostics with the time of any stutter.

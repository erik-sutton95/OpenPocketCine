New and changed

- Live View, camera controls, media and Multiview now share the OpenCine foundation, keeping Pocket's familiar monitor layout.
- View Assist keeps compact labels, familiar icons and matching monitoring options in Live View and playback.
- Diagnostic reports now separate incoming video, decoding and display stalls to help investigate live-view interruptions.

Fixes

- Pause stays paused during looping, seeking and scrubbing instead of unexpectedly resuming.
- Updated full-screen display setup for Android 15 and later.
- Release downloads now remove unused code and resources.

What to test

- Connect your camera, change recording settings and use View Assist in portrait and landscape. Check Multiview if you use more than one camera.
- In Media, pause a clip near its end, seek and scrub, then switch clips. Confirm Pause holds and playback resumes only when you ask.
- If the live picture drops out, note the time and use Connection setup > Share Diagnostics. Please include whether camera controls still respond.

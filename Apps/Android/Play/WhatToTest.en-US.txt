New and changed

- EV adds a movable exposure gauge in Auto, Manual and playback. It measures brightness relative to middle gray; D-Log M readings are marked as estimates.
- Motion Control loops through saved zooms and offers Restart while paused. Zoom controls stay accessible; D-Log2 blocks zoom-changing programs.
- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) offers lens presets and a custom 1.00–2.00× factor for live view, videos and photos.

Fixes

- EV waits for the new picture when switching playback clips instead of retaining the previous reading.
- Motion Control help text is brighter, the zoom dial retains fractional input, and loop reversals avoid false waypoint failures.
- Improved recovery when a running preview loses its video format.

What to test

- Enable EV in Auto and Manual, change light or exposure, then drag, resize and rotate. Try DISP 2 pins and playback. Confirm LUT changes do not alter EV.
- Save A/B/C at different zooms; try both loop directions, Pause/Resume and Restart. Drag the editor, change a duration, then close/reopen it. Check saved settings remain and actions stay fixed.
- Hold DE-SQ for presets, Custom and direction. Check framing and playback, then check preview recovery after Media, Settings and background use.

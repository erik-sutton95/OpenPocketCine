New and changed

- Report a problem is visible on the pairing screen, even before your first connection. Add a description, optional technical details and screenshots without opening Settings.
- TalkBack reads live-view readouts and assist tools as whole phrases, while preserving tap and hold actions.

Fixes

- Live view starts again without the low-latency setting when the phone's decoder refuses it, instead of staying black.
- Connection setup and stalled-picture recovery have bounded waits. Returning from Media starts live view once and avoids older recovery deadlines.

What to test

- Before connecting a camera, open Report a problem in portrait and landscape. Close it and confirm pairing is still available. Share Diagnostics remains in the pairing menu.
- Connect three times, then open Media and Settings, rotate and return to live view. Report any black picture or freeze with the time it happened.
- With TalkBack on, check REC, timecode, battery and assists each speak one phrase, and Report a problem is reachable before pairing.

New and changed

- A connected game controller's left stick drives the gimbal. Small throws crawl; full throw is fastest. Cross/A records. Circle/B recenters. Square/X is rotate-180. Triangle/Y tracks a face, or cancels tracking. L1/R1 jump zoom out/in. L2/R2 hold-to-zoom (press harder for faster). D-pad up/down ISO, left/right shutter. Connect and unplug should toast Gamepad connected / disconnected and rest the stick. Operator Setup → Controls → Gamepad shows Connected while the pad is on. A gimbal stop taps once after the head moves then stalls (Haptics off is silent).
- The on-screen gimbal stick uses the same gradual speed.
- While live, Operator Setup then Controls has a Shooting Mode row. Switch Video, Photo, and the time-based modes from there. The row follows a mode changed on the body itself.
- Pair over Bluetooth, join the camera's Wi-Fi, and watch a live view with waveform, parade, and assists.

Fixes

- Connecting or recovering live view should not close the app if the camera Wi-Fi drops or the phone jumps back to home Wi-Fi. You should see a retry or return to pairing.
- On a Nano, Photo now actually switches instead of staying on Video. Video back to Photo should ack and the shutter should take a still, not start a clip.
- Live view should come up in a couple of seconds after Wi-Fi joins, not sit on Waiting for live view.
- Live view should stay up while you pan or tilt. A frozen picture with a live timecode was over-repair. Stick notify now rides the ACK thread like iOS.
- Highlight zebra now paints when any color channel runs hot, not only when overall brightness does, so a blue-heavy hot sky stripes. Factory Highlight moved from 100 to 99 IRE. Saved thresholds are unchanged.

What to test

- Pair a Pocket, go live, and pan with the on-screen stick: half throw should be slower than a full push. Stay live two minutes with LUT on and pan — picture should not freeze while timecode still moves. Connect a game controller if you have one — left stick, Cross record, Circle recenter, Square 180, Triangle track, L1/R1 zoom jumps, L2/R2 hold-to-zoom, D-pad ISO/shutter, one buzz at a stop. On Pocket 4 Pro in D-Log2 (idle): R2 or pinch should switch COLOR to D-Log first, then zoom — the chip must not run ahead of the lens.
- Pair a Nano, go live, open Operator Setup, Controls. Switch Video to Photo and back. The body should follow; Photo should take a still.
- Record a short take in Video, then open it in the library. Share should send the original file, not a small preview.
- Pair a Pocket, go live, then turn camera Wi-Fi off and on or let the phone hop to home Wi-Fi. The app should retry or return to pairing — it must not close.

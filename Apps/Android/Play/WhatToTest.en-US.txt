New and changed

- The live top bar readouts (storage, format, mode, REC and timecode) get their dark glow back, so they stay readable over bright pictures.

Fixes

- After a long Media visit, the live picture comes back instead of the camera disconnecting.

What to test

- Point the camera at a bright or white scene and check that every top bar readout stays readable in portrait and landscape.
- Record a short clip, stay in Media for 30 minutes or more and return to Live View; the picture should come back. Report crashes or controls that stop responding.

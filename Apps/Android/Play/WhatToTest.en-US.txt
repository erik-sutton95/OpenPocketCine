New and changed

- Camera controls and Media now use the shared OpenCine screens, so they look and behave as in the other OpenCine camera apps.
- Exposure, shutter angle, frame-rate and storage readouts follow the camera's reported values; missing readings are marked unavailable.
- Recording duration follows confirmed camera recording state.

Fixes

- Fixed ML Kit initialization in minified Android releases to prevent a crash when live video starts.
- Pause remains available while playback waits for data; using it prevents unexpected restarts. Late loops, seeks and scrubs stay paused until you press Play.
- ISO controls follow the camera's declared settings, including its native ISO choices.
- Camera controls and Media use the connected camera's available settings.

What to test

- Connect your camera; keep live view running and try face tracking. Compare manual/auto exposure, ISO, shutter and storage with its screen. Report crashes after the first picture.
- Start/stop a short camera recording; check duration. In Media, try short/long clips, pause while buffering, scrub and switch. Restart only with Play; return to Live View.
- Reconnect and rotate; check missing readings and usable controls. For failed clips or live-view dropouts, share diagnostics, clip duration, cached status and whether controls responded.

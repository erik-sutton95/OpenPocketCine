New and changed

- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt gauges from the camera's own sensor, with a round bubble for top-down shots.
- Gimbal drawer → Double-tap → Level makes a joystick double-tap (and gamepad Circle/B) move the lens to world level or straight down.

What to test

- Turn on LEVEL and roll the handle in FPV. Check the roll gauge moves the right way and turns green only when level.
- Angle the handle forward, double-tap the joystick with Double-tap set to Level, and check the toast says Leveled top-down or how far off it stopped.
- In Multiview, try Record all and Live View and back.

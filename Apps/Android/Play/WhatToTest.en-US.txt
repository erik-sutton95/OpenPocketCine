New and changed

- UI 2.0 adds Sora, Lucide and adaptive pages. Tap or drag camera values to see the same full bottom-center drawer; gimbal Mode, Speed and Ramp have separate tabs.
- Pocket 4 Pro zoom: tap 1×/3×, double tap 6×/12×, hold for the lens dial.
- New scopes, LIGHTS and ND start centered until placed. AUDIO moves, supports vertical/horizontal bars and optional channel dB readings.

Fixes

- Glass is translucent, text shadows are tighter, and audio meters are slim. Wider View Assist options stay the same size between tabs; Record remains reachable above Zoom.
- App return checks camera Wi-Fi. Recovery waits for a new picture, shows its current stage and bounds automatic retries; old control requests cannot cross reconnects.
- Live rendering synchronizes completed frames with presentation. Gimbal input waits for the live picture; stalled rendering cannot count as recovery.
- Pocket 3 keeps portrait 3K selected while format options load and frame rate changes.

- On Pocket 4 Pro, compare still shots and slow pans with LUT/scopes off and on, especially on Redmi/Samsung. Share diagnostics with the time of any stutter.
- Check joystick after first picture, app return, Wi-Fi changes, Retry/Cancel and reconnects. Test Pocket 3 portrait 3K. Motion Control remains experimental.

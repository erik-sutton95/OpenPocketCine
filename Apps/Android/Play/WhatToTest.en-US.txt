New and changed

- UI 2.0 adds consistent buttons and signal colors, Media footer controls, pull-refresh and hold/sideways selection with vertical scrolling.
- Pocket 4 Pro zoom: tap 1×/3×, double tap 6×/12×, or hold for the lens dial at the screen edge.
- Scopes, LIGHTS and ND start centered until placed. AUDIO supports movable vertical/horizontal bars and optional channel dB readings.

Fixes

- Zoom factor, ND stop, exposure compensation and frame-rate readouts always print their decimals with a dot, whatever language the phone is set to.
- Pocket 3 FORMAT offers 16:9 and 1:1. Use body Lock Portrait for vertical recording unless the camera already reports 9:16.
- Pocket 3 shutter Speed and Angle work without a capability table; Speed no longer stays at 1/60.
- Photo sharing, Bluetooth drops and face auto-focus under load recover instead of closing the app.

What to test

- Set the phone to a language that writes decimals with a comma, then check the zoom factor, ND readings and exposure compensation still show a dot.
- On Pocket 3, switch 16:9/1:1 and shutter Speed/Angle. Check 16:9 guides on a 1:1 picture and confirm unsupported 9:16 does not hang.
- Rotate pages and try tap versus hold on camera values. Check assist tabs, audio, zoom, lock, Media selection and TalkBack.

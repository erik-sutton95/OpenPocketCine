New and changed

- HDR display (Operator Setup → Display) brightens live view, scopes, settings, media and playback on the phone panel. Off by default. This is not the camera's HDR/HLG color mode.
- Screen recording temporarily uses a normal SDR picture so the file is not HDR-boosted. WAVE / HISTO still read the decoded camera signal.
- Saved settings, remembered cameras and resized pictures now go through one shared path. Nothing should look or behave differently.

Fixes

- Screen recording on Android 15+ no longer keeps the HDR panel boost in the captured file.
- The app no longer closes on its own the moment it opens on Android 15 and newer phones.

What to test

- Turn on HDR display outdoors or at a bright window. Check the feed, WAVE, settings type, and a clip. Toggle it off and confirm the picture returns to normal.
- Start a screen recording with HDR display on and confirm the recording looks like a normal SDR picture, not the boosted panel.
- Change several Operator Setup options, force-stop the app and reopen it: every option should come back exactly as you left it, and a remembered camera should rejoin without asking again.

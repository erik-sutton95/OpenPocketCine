New and changed

- Pair over Bluetooth, join the camera's Wi-Fi, and watch a live view with waveform, parade, and assists.
- Record, ISO, white balance, and zoom from the phone. The gimbal stick sits with zoom.
- Open clips from the camera. LUT, false colour, peaking, and zebra grade the small preview. Share and Save still use the original camera file.

Fixes

- Live view should come up in a couple of seconds after Wi-Fi joins, not sit on Waiting for live view. White balance Auto keeps tint. Zoom chips match the body (4 Pro 1×/3×/6×/12×, Pocket 4 1×/2×/4×). FORMAT only lists what this camera can do. COLOR lists what this camera has: D-Log2 only on Pocket 4 Pro; Pocket 3 is D-Log M, not D-Log / D-Log2.

What to test

- Pair an Osmo Pocket 4 Pro, join its Wi-Fi, and confirm live view fills the monitor in a couple of seconds. COLOR should include D-Log2. On Pocket 3, COLOR is Normal / HDR / D-Log M — not D-Log2.
- Record a short take, then open it in the library with LUT on. Share should send the original file, not a small preview.
- Confirm the app installs on a 64-bit phone running Android 10 or newer.

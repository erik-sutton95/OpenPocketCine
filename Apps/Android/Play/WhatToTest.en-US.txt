New and changed

- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt meters from the camera's own sensor, plus Double-tap → Level in the gimbal drawer to move the lens to level or straight down.
- Osmo Action 6 support with live view, D-Log M and an APERTURE control. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.
- Scopes, paused playback and camera status updates use less power.

Fixes

- Deleting a clip from clip playback works again: Delete and Cancel no longer play or pause the clip behind the dialog.
- Live video no longer stops shortly after connecting while the app is busy.

What to test

- Turn on LEVEL and roll the handle in FPV: the roll meter should move with the picture. With Double-tap set to Level, angle the handle forward and double-tap.
- If you have an Action 6: pair it, confirm live view appears, then try APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.
- In Media, open a clip, tap the trash icon, and try Cancel, then Delete.

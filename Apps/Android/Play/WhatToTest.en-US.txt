New and changed

- EV adds a movable exposure gauge in Auto, Manual and playback. It measures brightness relative to middle gray; D-Log M readings are marked as estimates.
- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) offers lens presets and a custom 1.00–2.00× factor for live view, videos and photos.
- Report a problem remains available before pairing, with optional technical details and screenshots.

Fixes

- EV waits for the new picture when switching playback clips instead of retaining the previous reading.
- Improved recovery when a running preview loses its video format.

What to test

- Enable EV in Auto and Manual, change the light or exposure, then drag, resize and rotate. Try DISP 2 pins and playback. Confirm LUT changes do not alter EV.
- Hold DE-SQ for presets, Custom and direction. Check framing, rotation, playback zoom and saved settings.
- Open Report a problem before connecting. Then check preview recovery after Media, Settings and background use.

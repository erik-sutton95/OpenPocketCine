New and changed

- Motion Control loops through saved zooms and offers Restart while paused. The zoom chip and long-press disc stay accessible; D-Log2 blocks zoom-changing programs.
- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) works in live view, videos and photos, with lens presets and a custom 1.00–2.00× factor.

Fixes

- Auto exposure reads the camera's applied shutter under EV instead of the remembered manual shutter. Missing or unsupported readings leave only EV visible.
- Improved recovery when a running preview loses its video format.

What to test

- In Auto, change the lighting and compare the shutter under EV with the camera. Switch to Manual, select a shutter, then return to Auto and repeat.
- Save A/B/C at different zooms; try both loop directions, Pause/Resume and Restart. Drag the editor, adjust a duration, then close/reopen it and check saved settings remain.
- Try DE-SQ with LUTs, guides and playback. Check preview recovery after Media, Settings and background use.

New and changed

- UI 2.0: Sora/Lucide, consistent buttons and signal colors. Media footer controls, pull-refresh, and hold/sideways selection with native vertical scrolling.
- Pocket 4 Pro zoom: tap 1×/3×, double tap 6×/12×, hold for the larger lens dial extending to the screen edge above camera controls.
- New scopes, LIGHTS and ND start centered until placed. AUDIO moves, supports vertical/horizontal bars and optional channel dB readings.

Fixes

- Crash hardening: sharing a photo no longer closes the app, a Bluetooth drop recovers instead of crashing, and face auto-focus no longer aborts under load.
- Lock aligns with Settings. Portrait feed centers; STBY/timecode/REC SETUP sit below the cutout. View Assist/FIT/FILL/joystick stay fixed above camera values; motion editor keeps the stick usable.
- App return checks camera Wi-Fi. Recovery waits for a new picture, shows its current stage and bounds automatic retries; old control requests cannot cross reconnects.
- Live rendering synchronizes completed frames with presentation. Gimbal input waits for the live picture; stalled rendering cannot count as recovery.

What to test

- Check blur over bright/dark footage. Rotate pages; tap versus hold drums; open FORMAT in both orientations. Try assist tabs, audio, zoom, lock, Media swipe-vs-select, playback and TalkBack.
- On Pocket 4 Pro, compare still shots and slow pans with LUT/scopes off and on, especially on Redmi/Samsung. Share diagnostics with the time of any stutter.
- Drag Motion Control and use the joystick with its editor open. Check Wi-Fi changes, Retry/Cancel, reconnects and Pocket 3 portrait 3K. Motion Control remains experimental.

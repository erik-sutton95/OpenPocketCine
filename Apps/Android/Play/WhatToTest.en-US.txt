New and changed

- Pocket 3 FORMAT offers normal-video resolutions including 2.7K and square/vertical choices when the camera supplies no format list.
- D-Log M scopes use direct preview signal. Scene-stop readings use an estimated Pocket 3 curve, not measured sensor clipping limits.
- FALSE adds CineStop, EL Zone (+6 white / −6 black), and six-zone IRE. Tap ND for a suggested screw-on filter and stop count; it starts off and cannot set a filter.
- Motion Control adds timed A→B→C moves, smoothing, a three-second start countdown and Pause/Resume/Stop. Duration dials span 0.5–120 seconds; swipe left to increase. Dragging the editor suppresses button taps.
- Sharing remains Coming soon. Multiview is not available on Android in this build.

Fixes

- D-Log M waveform and zebras no longer use the D-Log black point or exposure ceiling.
- Shared frame assembly preserves complete large Nano pictures across transport groups. Android camera testing remains pending.
- Live picture stays sharp with LUT and PEAK. Face AF-C follows front and 3/4 views; settings and clips preserve the picture.
- Motion Control respects pan/tilt limits and stops on stale feedback or missed waypoints. Rehearse before a take.

What to test

- Pocket 3: select 2.7K at 25p in normal Video, confirm on the camera, record and reconnect. Camera-supplied format lists should take priority.
- In D-Log M, enable waveform, histogram and zebras, then change ISO. Check the scale stays stable. Compare CineStop, EL Zone and IRE; use IRE for signal measurements. Try ND on a bright take and check its reading changes with the picture.
- Watch Pocket/Nano live with LUT off/on and PEAK. Record, reconnect, track a face from front/3/4, then open settings or clips and return. Report freezes or corrupted pictures using Share Diagnostics. Pause VPNs if live view never starts.
- Pocket only: test the gimbal button, mode, speed and ramp. Set A/B and optional C; compare exact/smoothed moves. Test long durations, countdown cancellation, repeated Pause/Resume/Stop, and dragging without button activation. Stick or ActiveTrack should cancel the move.
- Confirm Operator Setup → Sharing still says Coming soon, then return to the live picture.

New and changed

- Dials, sliders and readouts carry their numbers with less work, leaving more room for live view to stay smooth.
- Pocket 3 zoom offers only the stops the recording FORMAT allows, with a short note naming the new cap.
- HDR display (Operator Setup → Display) boosts the phone panel, not the camera's HDR/HLG mode. Off by default; WAVE/HISTO still measure the camera signal.

Fixes

- The zoom readout settles on the camera's own value when a requested zoom is refused.
- Shutter, ISO, zoom and ND readouts use a fixed number format, including on phones whose language uses decimal commas.
- Screen recordings stay normal SDR, including on Android 15 and newer phones.
- The app no longer closes on its own the moment it opens on Android 15 and newer phones.

What to test

- Drag the zoom dial and scopes' brightness sliders. Check for stutters and unchanged readouts, including TalkBack on Operator Setup → Display.
- On Pocket 3, cycle zoom at 1080, 2.7K and 4K: caps are 4×, 3× and 2×. FORMAT changes return to 1×. Check shutter, ISO, zoom and ND on a decimal-comma phone language.
- Toggle HDR display in bright light. Check the feed, scopes, settings and playback, then make a screen recording and confirm the file stays normal SDR.

New and changed

- UI 2.0 adds Sora type, Lucide icons, adaptive camera/settings/media pages, and redesigned monitor controls for phones and tablets.
- Tap a camera value for its picker; hold or drag for a temporary drum. Hold Zoom for the lens dial. View Assist expands from a compact palette with scope previews.
- Gimbal Mode, Speed and Ramp use inline drums. Drag and resize scopes, LIGHTS and ND. Your cameras shows connection progress and Cancel.

Fixes

- Playback controls, clip info and sharing support accessibility focus. Record stays reachable above the zoom dial, and tablet controls avoid collisions.
- App return checks camera Wi-Fi. Recovery waits for a new picture, shows its current stage and bounds automatic retries; old control requests cannot cross reconnects.
- Live rendering synchronizes completed frames with presentation. Gimbal input waits for the live picture; stalled rendering cannot count as recovery.
- Pocket 3 keeps portrait 3K selected while format options load and frame rate changes.

What to test

- Rotate UI 2.0 pages and drawers on phones/tablets. Try value drums, View Assist, zoom, lock/unlock, media selection, playback and TalkBack.
- On Pocket 4 Pro, compare still shots and slow pans with LUT/scopes off and on, especially on Redmi/Samsung. Share diagnostics with the time of any stutter.
- Check joystick after first picture, app return, Wi-Fi changes, Retry/Cancel and reconnects. Test Pocket 3 portrait 3K. Motion Control remains experimental.

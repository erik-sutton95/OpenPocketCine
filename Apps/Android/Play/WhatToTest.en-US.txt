New and changed

- Your cameras shows connection progress and Cancel on the selected camera, with names above availability and connection buttons.
- Gimbal controls has Mode, Speed, and Ramp tabs, plus Motion Control in Gimbal tools. Direction Lock holds the pointing direction as the handle rotates.
- Hold-drag the ND chip to move it. Long-press Units for Stops, filter factor, or optical density.

Fixes

- Pocket 3 keeps vertical 3K in FORMAT while options load and when changing frame rate.

What to test

- Reconnect a saved camera, check progress on its row, and cancel. Check Your cameras in portrait and landscape.
- Switch gimbal tabs, try Direction Lock then Follow, and open Motion Control. Confirm the live picture stays smooth.
- On Pocket 3, change frame rate in vertical 3K and confirm FORMAT stays 3K 9:16. Move the ND chip and cycle Units.

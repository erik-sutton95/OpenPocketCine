New and changed

- Dials, sliders and readouts use less memory while preserving their values.
- Pocket 3 zoom follows FORMAT limits and refused values settle back. Shutter, ISO, zoom and ND use consistent formatting across phone languages.
- HDR display brightens the phone panel; off by default. Screen recording uses normal SDR. Camera HDR/HLG recording is unchanged.

Fixes

- Connection setup waits for the camera's command window. Recovery can act sooner after lost video data while keeping the last picture.
- Replacement keyframes stay protected. First-picture recovery is bounded; repeated controls cannot indefinitely delay a stalled decoder or frame assembly.
- Media return starts live view once. Older picture-repair deadlines no longer reconnect just because you opened Media.
- Android 15+ startup and HDR screen-recording fixes remain included.

What to test

- Connect three times, then run LUT/WAVE for five minutes while using zoom and scope brightness sliders. Share diagnostics and the time of slow startup or freezes.
- Open Media, play a clip and return repeatedly. Cover Settings, rotate and briefly leave the app; confirm picture returns.
- On Pocket 3, check 1080 zooms to 4×, 2.7K to 3× and 4K to 2×. Check readouts in a decimal-comma language and normal SDR screen recordings with HDR display on.

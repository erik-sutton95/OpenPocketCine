New and changed

- Long-press FALSE → Scale now includes EL Zone: 15 stop colors from 18% gray. +6 and brighter is white; −6 and darker is black.
- Live PEAK paints a closed outline on sharp edges, including with LUT on.
- Face AF-C finds 3/4 views, not only eyes-front, and the green box sits on the person.

Fixes

- Live picture is no longer blocky with LUT on or off.
- WAITING FOR LIVE VIEW should not sit on screen after Wi-Fi joins.
- Opening Operator Setup or clips, then back, keeps the live picture (not a black well).
- Face lock is closer to iOS: it tracks the face without grabbing every oval, and the box is not mirrored onto the wrong side.

What to test

- Pair a Pocket 4 Pro, join Wi-Fi, and go live. Long-press FALSE, pick EL Zone: mid-gray is gray, a hot sky goes white. Toggle LUT on and off and look for a smooth picture, not a grid. Turn PEAK on and off.
- With AF-C, the green face box should sit on the person (front and 3/4). Tap it to track; cancel with the X. Open Operator Setup, then back: the picture must still be there.
- Stay live two minutes with LUT on. Record, FORMAT, COLOR, or a tracking box must not freeze the picture. Pause VPNs or ad blockers if live view never starts.

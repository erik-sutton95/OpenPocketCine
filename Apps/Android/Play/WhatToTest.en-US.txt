New and changed

- HDR display in Operator Setup → Display brightens the phone panel. Off by default; it does not change camera HDR/HLG recording.

Fixes

- Connection setup waits for the camera's command window. Recovery can act sooner after lost video data while keeping the last picture.
- Replacement keyframes stay protected. First-picture recovery is bounded; repeated controls cannot indefinitely delay a stalled decoder or frame assembly.
- Android 15+ startup and HDR screen-recording fixes remain included.
- Media return starts live view once. Older picture-repair deadlines no longer reconnect just because you opened Media.

What to test

- Connect and disconnect three times, then run for five minutes with LUT/WAVE and normal camera controls. Share diagnostics and the time of slow startup or any freeze.
- Open Media, play a clip and return repeatedly. Check that live picture resumes and no reconnect interrupts browsing.
- Cover and uncover Settings, rotate, and briefly leave the app. Confirm the picture returns. With HDR display on, screen recordings should remain normal SDR.

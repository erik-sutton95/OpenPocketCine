New and changed

- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) works in live view, video playback and photos, with lens presets and a custom 1.00–2.00× factor.
- Report a problem is visible on the pairing screen before your first connection, with optional technical details and screenshots.

Fixes

- Auto exposure reads the camera's applied shutter under EV instead of the remembered manual shutter. Missing or unsupported readings leave only EV visible.
- Improved recovery when a running preview loses its video format.

What to test

- In Auto, change the lighting and compare the shutter under EV with the camera. Switch to Manual, select a shutter, then return to Auto and repeat.
- Tap DE-SQ to toggle; hold for presets, Custom and direction. Check live view and playback with LUTs and grids.
- Open Report a problem before pairing. Then connect and check preview recovery after Media, Settings and background use.

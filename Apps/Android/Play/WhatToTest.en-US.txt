Since open beta build 138

New and changed

- Record sends start or stop for the camera's reported state, never a blind toggle, and waits for that state after connecting.
- Multiview Record all rechecks every camera when you confirm and shows each tile's result; one failure stops no other. Photo mode disables it.
- New CineStop false color: greens below 18% gray, gray at 18%, pink +1 skin, yellow +2 upper skin, red clip. Old scale is now Video; Read picks 709 or LOG.
- Long-press MIRROR for Horizontal and Vertical. Both on show an underslung camera, such as on a car mount, the right way up.
- Long-press the picture to lock exposure (AE-L); the sun-and-lock key beside Recenter unlocks. WB Mode adds AWB Lock.
- Tabs use one highlighted edge and tool buttons share one size.
- Live View indicators are glass pills for camera battery, phone battery and link; tap the link pill for feed fps.
- Double-tap the picture to start object tracking, as on the camera. Joystick size: Small, Medium or Large in Settings, Controls.
- Experimental Multiview: up to four Osmo cameras on shared Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot, from the grid button on Your cameras.
- Multiview redesign: bigger main picture, camera strip, Live View's toolbar and a Camera settings side panel.
- Wi-Fi and Hotspot setup uses choice cards, grouped networks, checklists and a pinned Connect button.
- Saved cameras can connect over your Wi-Fi or this phone's hotspot: Add setup on the camera card, then switch with its chips.
- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt meters from the camera's sensor, with a bubble for top-down shots.
- Gimbal drawer Double-tap: Recenter, or Level to move the lens level or straight down. Experimental.
- Osmo Action 6: live view, D-Log M with its LUT, Photo, Timelapse and APERTURE. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.
- Live View uses about 60% less CPU with a LUT, PEAK or WAVE on a Galaxy S25.

Fixes

- Multiview tiles and playback scopes each keep their own WAVE 100 and false color clip.
- The Multiview record confirmation closes when a camera reconnects or changes.
- Traffic Lights no longer flag clipping just under clip; zebra at 100% marks clipped highlights.
- Pairing finds the camera with only Nearby devices allowed (Location on Android 10 and 11). If Android stops asking, Allow opens Settings.
- Pairing reminds you to turn off Frame Tap and force quit DJI Mimo.
- The gimbal joystick moves as fast on diagonals as straight. AE lock gives a stronger double tap.
- Portrait Live View shows Recenter focus after an off-centre tap.
- Media lists complete SD and internal libraries with capacity visible. Playback stays paused during a loop, seek or scrub.
- Shutter angle stays at your chosen value when changing frame rate or opening the picker.
- A new track no longer flashes on a previously tracked face.
- Multiview Exit retries a temporary pairing refusal before showing an error.
- Wi-Fi setup fields align and Connect stays above the keyboard.
- Deleting a clip from playback works again.
- Live View shows the whole picture with the camera held upright, instead of a heavily zoomed crop.
- Osmo Nano no longer shows zoom controls.
- Live View no longer freezes for seconds after one dropped video packet.
- Live View no longer crashes on MediaTek phones or with gimbal controls on some screens.

What to test

- Record with and without record confirmation from the lamp and a gamepad: the camera starts or stops once. Right after connecting, Record waits.
- In Multiview, Record all on 2 to 4 cameras: each tile changes once and shows its result. Reconnect a tile with the confirmation open: it closes.
- Two Multiview tiles at different ISO, and playback scopes while live, keep their own WAVE 100 and false color clip.
- Browse many clips or both storage types, and Nano after playback. Pause in a loop or scrub: it must hold. Change FPS at 180°: the angle stays 180°.
- Turn on LEVEL and roll the handle in FPV. On Nano, the whole picture shows upright with no zoom chip.

New and changed

- Pocket 3 FORMAT offers its documented normal-video resolutions, including 2.7K and square/vertical choices, when the camera does not supply a format list.
- D-Log M scopes use a direct preview-signal scale. DLM approximate stop readings use an estimated Pocket 3 curve; they do not identify measured sensor clipping limits.

Fixes

- D-Log M waveform and zebras no longer use the D-Log black point or exposure ceiling.
- Shared frame assembly now preserves complete large Nano pictures across transport groups. Android Nano smoothness still needs camera testing.

What to test

- On Pocket 3 in normal Video, select 2.7K at 25p, confirm the camera setting, record a short clip and reconnect. Camera-supplied format lists should still take priority.
- Enable D-Log M waveform, histogram and zebras, then change ISO. Check the signal scale does not jump to D-Log limits. Use IRE for signal measurements; approximate stop readings need further calibration.
- With Pocket and Nano cameras, watch live view with LUT off and on, record a short clip, then reconnect. Report freezes and corrupted pictures using Share Diagnostics. Multiview is not available on Android in this build.

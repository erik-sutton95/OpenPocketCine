New and changed

- Live stream to Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or a Custom server: Settings > Sharing > Live stream, then Go live under the timecode.
- Hold zoom for the zoom panel: a ruler for smooth zoom, three presets and a Preset ramp. Pocket 4 Pro reaches 18x on DJI's November 2026 firmware.
- Camera presets, first on the Settings Controls tab, save and recall exposure and white balance.

Fixes

- The monitor lock also holds the screen rotation, so a phone mounted upside down no longer turns when the rig is levelled.
- Multiview tiles show REC with the time or STBY, and the settings popup stays open across a record start or stop.
- The photo viewer has the same View Assist bar as clips, with peaking, false colour and zebra.
- A Pocket 4 Pro that reports an unfamiliar Wi-Fi mode joins a Wi-Fi or Hotspot setup instead of being refused.

What to test

- Save a stream key, connect over the phone's hotspot and tap Go live. Check the picture on the service, then Stop.
- Hold zoom, drag the ruler, save three presets, set a Preset ramp of 2 s and tap between them.
- Save a Camera preset, change ISO, shutter and white balance, then recall it.

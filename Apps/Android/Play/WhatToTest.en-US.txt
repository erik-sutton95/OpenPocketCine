New and changed

- Shared diagnostics include picture timings and drop estimates to help distinguish a delayed feed from a jumpy one.
- TalkBack reads live-view readouts and assist tools as whole phrases, while preserving tap and hold actions.
- Pocket 3 zoom follows FORMAT limits and refused values settle back. Shutter, ISO, zoom and ND use consistent formatting across phone languages.

Fixes

- Connection setup waits for the camera's command window. Recovery can act sooner after lost video data while keeping the last picture.
- Replacement keyframes stay protected. First-picture recovery is bounded; repeated controls cannot indefinitely delay a stalled decoder or frame assembly.
- Media return starts live view once. Older picture-repair deadlines no longer reconnect just because you opened Media.
- Bluetooth setup advances through supported notification fallbacks and reports rejected setup writes promptly, with more useful diagnostics.

What to test

- Connect three times. If Bluetooth fails, retry once and share diagnostics. Test five minutes of live view; report the time of any freeze and whether the picture felt late or jumpy.
- Open Media, play a clip and return repeatedly. Cover Settings, rotate and briefly leave the app; confirm picture returns.
- With TalkBack on, check REC, timecode, battery and assists each speak one phrase. Double-tap and hold an assist tool to check both actions.

New and changed

- No new controls this build. Existing camera-side subject tracking is unchanged.
- Pocket 3 zoom stops follow FORMAT, with a note when the ceiling changes. Refused zoom values settle back to the camera reading.

Fixes

- Live view keeps the last picture during recovery. Connection setup waits for the camera command window, and repeated controls cannot delay stalled-picture recovery indefinitely.
- TalkBack announces the live readouts. Android 15 and later keep launch working without the recording callback.

What to test

- Connect and disconnect three times, then run five minutes with LUT/WAVE and ordinary controls. Share diagnostics and the time of slow startup or any freeze.
- On Pocket 3, check 1080 zooms to 4×, 2.7K to 3× and 4K to 2×. Enable TalkBack and check live readouts remain understandable.
- Open Media, play a clip and return repeatedly. Confirm the live picture resumes.

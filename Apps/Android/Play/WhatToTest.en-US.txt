New and changed

- Motion Control Loop repeats the saved take until Stop, returning to A between runs. Closing and reopening the editor keeps the program for the camera session.
- TalkBack reads live-view readouts and assist tools as whole phrases, while preserving tap and hold actions.
- Pocket 3 zoom follows FORMAT limits and refused values settle back. Readouts use consistent formatting across phone languages.

Fixes

- Bluetooth setup reports rejected writes promptly. Connection and picture recovery use bounded retries; Media return starts live view once.

What to test

- Set A/B and optional C, enable Loop, and watch several repeats. Test Pause/Resume and Stop during the take and return to A. Try Smoothness with C set.
- Close and reopen Motion Control. Check points, durations, Smoothness and Loop remain. Clear should remove points and switch Loop off.
- Connect repeatedly and return from Media. With TalkBack on, check each readout speaks one phrase and assist tap/hold still work. Check Pocket 3 zoom limits per FORMAT.

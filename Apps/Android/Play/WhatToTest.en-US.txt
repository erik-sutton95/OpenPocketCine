New and changed

- TalkBack reads the live view readouts as whole phrases: "Recording, 2 minutes 14 seconds", "Camera battery 87 percent", "Waveform, on. Hold for options".
- HDR display (Operator Setup → Display) brightens live view, scopes, settings, media and playback on the phone panel. Off by default. This is not the camera's HDR/HLG color mode.

Fixes

- Assist tools and readouts no longer read their on-screen abbreviation a second time after their name.
- Screen recording on Android 15+ no longer keeps the HDR panel boost in the captured file.
- The app no longer closes on its own the moment it opens on Android 15 and newer phones.

What to test

- With TalkBack on, swipe through the live view: REC chip, timecode, battery, FPS and the assist row. Each should be one stop that speaks one sentence.
- Double-tap an assist tool, then press and hold it. Both the tap and the hold must still work with TalkBack on.
- Turn on HDR display outdoors or at a bright window. Check the feed, WAVE, settings type, and a clip. Toggle it off and confirm the picture returns to normal.

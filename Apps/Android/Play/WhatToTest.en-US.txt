New features

- Experimental Motion Control: plan timed A→B or A→B→C gimbal moves with smoothing, a start countdown, and Pause/Resume/Stop.
- New false-color choices: CineStop, EL Zone and six-zone IRE.
- ND assist suggests a screw-on filter strength and shows the stop count to help balance exposure.
- Expanded FORMAT choices show camera-reported resolutions, frame rates and aspect ratios, with normal-video options including 2.7K for Pocket 3.

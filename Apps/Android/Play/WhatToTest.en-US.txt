New and changed

- Direction Lock replaces Locked in the gimbal menu. It holds the camera's pointing direction as you rotate the handle. Choose Follow to release it.
- Hold-drag the ND chip to move it. Long-press Units for Stops, filter factor, or optical density.
- False color offers EL Zone, IRE, and CineStop scales for checking exposure.

Fixes

- Pocket 3 keeps vertical 3K in FORMAT while options load and when changing frame rate.

What to test

- Select Direction Lock and rotate the handle, then return to Follow. Check the menu follows camera mode changes and the live picture stays smooth.
- Set Pocket 3 to vertical 3K, connect, and change frame rate. Confirm FORMAT stays 3K 9:16.
- Move the ND chip and cycle Units. Switch false-color scales and check that the picture and waveform continue updating.

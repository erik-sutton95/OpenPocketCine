New and changed

- Recovery shows the current connection stage and keeps Retry and Operator menu available. Share Diagnostics includes more detail about uneven live video.
- Gimbal controls use Mode, Speed and Ramp tabs. Drag and resize scopes, LIGHTS and ND; Your cameras shows progress and Cancel.

Fixes

- App return checks camera Wi-Fi and reconnects when needed. Recovery waits for a new picture and stops automatic retries after a bounded interval.
- Reconnect starts a fresh camera session; old control requests cannot carry over. Gimbal input waits for the live picture; stalled rendering cannot count as recovery.
- Live rendering now synchronizes the completed frame with presentation. Please check motion smoothness on your phone.
- Pocket 3 keeps portrait 3K selected while format options load and frame rate changes.

What to test

- On Pocket 4 Pro, compare static shots and slow pans with LUT/scopes off and on, especially on Redmi and Samsung. Share diagnostics with the time of any stutter.
- Try joystick and gimbal modes immediately after first picture. Leave the app for 15 and 60 seconds; check recovery after a camera Wi-Fi change or camera power cycle.
- Try Retry, Cancel and reconnect repeatedly. Check portrait 3K on Pocket 3 and the gimbal menu. Motion Control remains experimental.

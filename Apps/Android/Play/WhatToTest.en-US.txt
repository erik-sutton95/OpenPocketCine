Since open beta build 138

New and changed

- False color code cleanup.
- Long-press MIRROR for Horizontal and Vertical. Turn both on to see an underslung camera, such as on a car mount, the right way up.
- Long-press the picture to lock exposure: the focus box turns yellow with AE-L. The sun-and-lock key beside Recenter unlocks. WB Mode adds AWB Lock.
- Tabs use one highlighted edge instead of boxes and tool buttons share one size.
- Live View indicators are glass pills for camera battery, phone battery and link. Tap the link pill to swap signal and feed fps.
- Joystick size: Small, Medium or Large in Settings, Controls.
- Double-tap the picture to start object tracking, as on the camera.
- Experimental Multiview: the grid button on Your cameras monitors up to four Osmo cameras on shared Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot, with Auto LUT and per-tile or group recording.
- Multiview redesign: bigger main picture, camera strip, Live View's toolbar and a Camera settings side panel.
- Wi-Fi and Hotspot setup uses choice cards, grouped networks, checklists and a pinned Connect button.
- Saved cameras can also connect over your Wi-Fi or this phone's hotspot: tap Add setup on the camera card, then switch with its setup chips.
- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt meters from the camera's own sensor, with a bubble for top-down and straight-up shots.
- Gimbal drawer Double-tap: keep Recenter, or choose Level so a joystick or gamepad double-tap moves the lens to level or straight down. Experimental.
- Osmo Action 6 support: live view, D-Log M with the official LUT, Photo, Timelapse and a live APERTURE tile. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.
- Live View uses about 60% less CPU with a LUT, PEAK or WAVE on a Galaxy S25.
- Devices without Google Play can install the APK from GitHub Releases.

Fixes

- Pairing finds the camera with only Nearby devices allowed (Location on Android 10 and 11). If Android stops asking, Allow opens Settings.
- Pairing reminds you to turn off DJI Frame Tap and force quit DJI Mimo.
- The gimbal joystick moves as fast on diagonals as straight up, down or sideways. AE lock gives a stronger double tap.
- Portrait Live View shows Recenter focus after an off-centre tap. The zoom chip, joystick and gimbal controls have the dark glow of other controls.
- Media lists complete SD and internal-storage libraries and keeps capacity visible during playback.
- Shutter angle stays at your chosen value when changing frame rate. Opening the picker no longer changes the saved angle.
- A new track no longer flashes on a previously tracked face.
- Multiview Exit retries a temporary pairing refusal before showing an error.
- Wi-Fi headings and fields align, Connect stays above the keyboard, and connecting or cancelling waits for scan cleanup.
- Deleting a clip from clip playback works again: Delete and Cancel no longer play or pause the clip behind the dialog.
- Osmo Nano no longer shows zoom controls, and DISP settings list Zoom Chip and Gimbal Stick only when the camera has them.
- Live View no longer freezes for seconds after one dropped video packet. The picture returns with the camera's next keyframe.
- Live View no longer crashes on MediaTek phones or on some screen sizes with gimbal controls shown.

What to test

- Open Media on a camera with many clips or both storage types. On Nano, open it right after playback starts. Confirm all clips and capacity appear.
- In Multiview, try Grid and Center stage in both orientations, open Camera settings, and switch apps to check feeds resume. Exit once.
- Double-tap an object to track it, with a face in the scene. Set the joystick to Large. Set 180° and change FPS: angle should stay 180°.
- Turn on LEVEL and roll the handle in FPV. In Media, open a clip, tap the trash icon, and try Cancel, then Delete.
- On Nano, confirm there is no zoom chip. If you have an Action 6, check live view, APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.

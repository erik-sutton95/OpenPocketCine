New and changed

- In Auto, the EV sheet has Face Priority. On, the drum grays out and EV follows faces to middle gray — fast for the first couple of seconds a face is in, then about one third-stop a second.
- The EV tile keeps the number and puts a small face mark on the EV label.
- Saved cameras keep the camera Wi-Fi when you leave the app, so reconnect should not re-prompt every time.
- A look that was already on should grade the first picture instead of popping in seconds later.
- ISO has an Auto Native ISO switch. Off keeps your ISO when you change D-Log / D-Log2.
- The SHUTTER sheet has Speed and Angle tabs. Angle runs 5.6° to 360° and is sent as the matching 1/N.

Fixes

- Traffic Lights crush/clip compensation starts at 0 stops. Long-press LIGHTS if you want the old ¼-stop tolerance.
- First connect no longer sits on Waiting for live-view when a few video packets arrive and then freeze.
- While a subject is tracked, stick left/right matches the free gimbal instead of reversing.
- Turn Face Priority off and EV returns to the value from before it was on, or 0.0.
- After leaving a clip, a dead picture with live camera status rebuilds the socket in about two seconds instead of sitting black for 15.
- The live feed comes up as soon as the camera answers, instead of waiting out extra beats.
- If the camera is still talking but the picture dies, the app asks for a fresh frame instead of sitting on a black well.

What to test

- Set MODE to Auto, open EV, turn Face Priority on. Confirm the EV number is readable, the face mark sits on the EV label, and a face in a dark or bright frame moves EV quickly for a couple of seconds, then about once a second.
- Put a face in frame, then turn Face Priority off — EV should return to the value from before it was on (or 0.0).
- Open a clip, leave it, and confirm live view returns in a couple of seconds — not a long black well.
- On ISO, turn Auto Native ISO off, switch D-Log ↔ D-Log2, and confirm ISO stays put.
- In Manual, open SHUTTER, switch to Angle, pick 180°, and confirm the tile shows 180°.

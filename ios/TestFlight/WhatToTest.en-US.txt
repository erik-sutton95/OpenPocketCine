New and changed

- HDR display (Operator Setup → Display) brightens live view, scopes, settings, media and playback on the phone panel. Off by default. This is not the camera's HDR/HLG color mode.
- Screen recording and AirPlay temporarily use a normal SDR picture so the file is not HDR-boosted. WAVE / HISTO still read the decoded camera signal.

Fixes

- HDR live view is no longer flipped vertically on the Metal present path.

What to test

- Turn on HDR display outdoors or at a bright window. Check the feed, WAVE, settings type, and a clip. Toggle it off and confirm the picture returns to normal.
- Start a screen recording with HDR display on and confirm the recording looks like a normal SDR picture, not the boosted panel.
- Keep a live session running while changing settings and zooming. Share diagnostics with the time of any freeze.

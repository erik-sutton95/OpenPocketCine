New features

- Your cameras: tap Add setup, choose Wi-Fi or Hotspot, and check the camera goes live on that network.
- Wi-Fi should list nearby networks by itself within about 20 seconds; Hotspot should fill in this iPhone's name. Check the progress bar while connecting.
- If a connect fails, try Edit setup, Try again and Camera Wi-Fi. Tap Camera Wi-Fi afterwards and check the camera returns to its own Wi-Fi.
- Multiview's Wi-Fi join was reorganized: add two or more cameras and confirm preview, recovery, and return to camera Wi-Fi on close.

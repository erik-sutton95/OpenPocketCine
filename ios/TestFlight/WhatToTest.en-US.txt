New and changed

- HDR display (Operator Setup → Display) brightens live view, scopes, settings, media and playback on the phone panel. Off by default. This is not the camera's HDR/HLG color mode.
- Pocket 3 FORMAT follows the camera's reported choices. Square frame guides follow the recorded picture.

Fixes

- Improved live-picture handling when switching between a camera and Multiview, rotating or resizing the app.
- Capture-mode changes, playback assists, proxy downloads and media-cache work include the previous Sentry crash and hang fixes.

What to test

- Switch between a Multiview tile and its single-camera monitor, rotate or resize the app, and confirm the picture continues. Share diagnostics with the time of any freeze.
- Try cold AI upscaling, playback assists and a populated Media library. Confirm the interface responds while caching or clearing files.
- Turn HDR display on, then start screen recording and confirm it stays normal SDR. On Pocket 3, check FORMAT and square framing against the camera.

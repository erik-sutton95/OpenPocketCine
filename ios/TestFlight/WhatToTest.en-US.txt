New and changed

- Share this feed in Operator Setup → Sharing sends the live picture to nearby OpenPocketCine iPhones and iPads. Watchers do not join camera Wi-Fi. Set an optional passcode and allow control requests.
- Broadcast priority sets the quality ceiling. Sharing can lower its bitrate when the host picture slows.

Fixes

- Joining and camera readouts no longer crowd out the shared picture.
- Sharing limits pending video work to prevent growing delay. A slow watcher waits for a fresh keyframe while other watchers continue.
- Leaving and rejoining ignores callbacks from the previous connection.

What to test

- Note host FPS with sharing off. Turn sharing on, join one watcher, then two or more. Pan and record for five minutes with LUT and waveform on. Check both host and watchers for stutter or growing delay.
- Move one watcher out of range, then return it. The host and other watchers should keep moving; the returning picture should resume.
- Stop and restart sharing. Leave and rejoin a feed. Try a wrong passcode, then the correct one. Request control, grant it, and reclaim it on the host.
- Try each Broadcast priority setting with several watchers. If the picture slows, share diagnostics and report device models, watcher count, and whether the host or watchers slowed first.

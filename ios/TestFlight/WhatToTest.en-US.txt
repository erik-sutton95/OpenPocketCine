New and changed

- Report a problem is visible on the pairing screen, even before your first connection. Add a description, optional technical details and screenshots without opening Settings.
- Pocket 3 zoom follows FORMAT limits. HDR display offers an optional outdoor screen boost; screen recording and AirPlay stay SDR.

Fixes

- Connection setup and stalled-picture recovery have bounded waits. An established feed keeps its last picture while recovering.
- Returning from Media starts live view once; older recovery deadlines no longer reconnect just because you opened Media.

What to test

- Before connecting a camera, open Report a problem in portrait and landscape. Close it and confirm pairing is still available. Share Diagnostics remains in the pairing menu.
- Connect three times, then open Media and Settings, rotate and return to live view. Report any slow startup or freeze with the time it happened.
- On Pocket 3, check 1080 zooms to 4×, 2.7K to 3× and 4K to 2×. With HDR display on, confirm screen recordings remain normal SDR.

New and changed

- Apple Watch companion: live preview, timecode, storage, camera battery, and record/shutter. Keep the iPhone app open; wrist recording skips confirmation.
- Share's Convert log rewrites D-Log and D-Log2 clips to one output curve. Camera originals stay untouched.
- Hold-drag the ND assist chip to move it. Long-press Units to choose Stops, filter factor, or optical density.

Fixes

- Watch preview has lower delay and matches the phone's identity picture. Always On keeps the last frame visible with normal watchOS dimming.
- Pocket 3 keeps vertical 3K in FORMAT while options load and when changing frame rate.

What to test

- Go live on iPhone and open OpenPocketCine on Apple Watch. Check preview and readings, record a short take, then lower and raise your wrist. Confirm recording on the camera.
- On Pocket 3, select vertical 3K, connect, then change frame rate. Confirm FORMAT stays 3K 9:16.
- Export mixed D-Log/D-Log2 takes with Convert log. In live view, move the ND chip and cycle its Units choices.

New and changed

- A connected game controller's left stick drives the gimbal; small throws crawl, full throw is fastest. Cross/A records. Circle/B recenters. Square/X is rotate-180. Triangle/Y tracks a face or cancels tracking. L1/R1 jump zoom, L2/R2 hold-to-zoom, D-pad ISO and shutter. Connect and unplug toast and rest the stick. A gimbal stop taps once (Haptics off is silent).
- Head tracking is experimental: Operator Setup → Controls, turn Head Tracking (Experimental) on. Wear AirPods with motion, go live, look forward, hold still, tap Calibrate Head Lock. That pose is shared zero: a 53° head turn pans the Pocket 53°. Nod is tilt. Roll is numbers only. STOP clears the lock. Off keeps AirPods as audio only. Allow Motion & Fitness if iOS asks.
- The on-screen gimbal stick uses the same gradual speed.
- Pair over Bluetooth, join the camera's Wi-Fi, and watch a live view. Record, ISO, white balance, and zoom from the phone.
- Operator Setup → System → Share Diagnostics. A TestFlight screenshot copies a short paste for the feedback comment. No name or location.

Fixes

- In D-Log2, zoom waits for the hop to D-Log before the lens moves; the chip stays at 1× until color switches. Zoom keeps the live picture, not just HUD and stick. COLOR lists only what this camera has. Live view should come up a couple of seconds after Wi-Fi joins, not sit on Waiting for live view.
- Live view should stay up while you pan, tilt, or use head tracking. Record, FORMAT, COLOR, white balance, or a tracking box must not freeze the picture. If it dies, the last frame stays under Reconnecting and live view should return without closing the app.
- Head tracking no longer bobs past where you look and swings back. Turning your head, then nodding, tilts straight up and down. Rapid look-pause-look gestures no longer freeze the picture.
- Power-cycling the camera mid-session keeps the last picture under the Reconnecting card instead of going black.
- With LUT, waveform, peaking, or Face AF on, zooming or the D-Log2 to D-Log hop no longer freeze the picture.
- Opening clips or Operator Setup over live view, then going back, keeps the live picture while the camera still responds.
- On Pocket 3 and Pocket 4, Auto ISO ranges now start at 50, matching the camera. Pocket 4 Pro still starts at 100.
- Zebra: Highlight now paints when any color channel runs hot, so a blue-heavy hot sky stripes. Factory Highlight moved from 100 to 99 IRE; saved thresholds are unchanged. Long-press ZEBRA, tap Highlight or Midtone: the number field stays above the keyboard and Done dismisses the pad; tap outside still closes them.

What to test

- Pair a Pocket, go live, pan with the stick (half throw slower than full). Stay live two minutes with LUT and waveform on. Zoom, record, switch FORMAT or COLOR, long-press-track a moving subject. Frozen picture with live timecode is a bug. If Reconnecting appears, last picture stays and live view returns without closing the app.
- Connect a DualSense or any extended gamepad. Left stick moves the gimbal. Cross records. Circle recenters. Square does 180. Triangle tracks a face. L1/R1 jump zoom. L2/R2 hold-to-zoom. D-pad ISO and shutter. Push into a stop: one buzz, not a rumble. Unplug rests the stick and toasts.
- On Pocket 4 Pro in D-Log2 (idle): press R2 or pinch. COLOR switches to D-Log first, then the picture zooms. On a Pocket 3, open ISO and switch to Auto: ranges read 50–400 and up, same as the camera. Pocket 4 Pro still starts at 100.
- Record a short take, open it in the library with LUT on. Share sends the original file. Close the library, then Operator Setup: live view still shows a picture. Power the Pocket off and on: the last picture stays under Reconnecting. If live view fails, Share Diagnostics (or screenshot and paste into TestFlight).
- Go live, long-press ZEBRA, tap Highlight, type 95, tap Done: the stripes update. Repeat in landscape. In D-Log2 at factory Highlight, a hot sky stripes. Your threshold is kept.

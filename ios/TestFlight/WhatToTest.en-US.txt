New and changed

- Playback controls follow the player's real playing and buffering state.
- Diagnostic reports separate incoming video, decoding and display stalls to help investigate live-view interruptions.

Fixes

- Media clip taps stay usable after a camera source changes or reconnects.
- Pause remains available while playback waits for data; pressing it prevents an unexpected restart.

What to test

- Reconnect your camera, open Media and tap a clip. Try short and long clips, then switch clips during playback.
- Pause while a clip is buffering, then scrub or return to Media. Playback should restart only when you press Play.
- If a clip will not open or live view drops out, share diagnostics and include its duration, whether it was cached and whether camera controls still responded.

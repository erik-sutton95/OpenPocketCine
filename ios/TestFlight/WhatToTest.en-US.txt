New and changed

- Direction Lock replaces Locked in the gimbal menu. It holds the camera's pointing direction as you rotate the handle. Choose Follow to release it.
- Apple Watch shows live preview, timecode, storage, and battery, with record/shutter controls. Keep the iPhone app open.
- Share's Convert log rewrites D-Log and D-Log2 clips to one output curve. Camera originals stay untouched.

Fixes

- Pocket 3 keeps vertical 3K in FORMAT while options load and when changing frame rate.

What to test

- Select Direction Lock and rotate the handle, then return to Follow. Check the menu follows camera mode changes and the live picture stays smooth. Mode selection switches off AirPods head tracking.
- Go live on iPhone and open the Watch companion. Check preview and readings, record a short take, then lower and raise your wrist.
- On Pocket 3, change frame rate in vertical 3K and confirm FORMAT stays 3K 9:16. Export mixed D-Log/D-Log2 takes with Convert log.

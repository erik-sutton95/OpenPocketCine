New and changed

- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) works in live view, video playback and photos. Choose a lens preset or a custom 1.00–2.00× factor in 0.01 steps.
- Report a problem is visible on the pairing screen, even before your first connection, with optional technical details and screenshots.

Fixes

- Improved recovery when a running preview loses its video format.
- Opening Control Center or a system alert no longer drops the preview after about 10 seconds, and stalled video now recovers in about 2 seconds.
- Returning from another app keeps the camera link, and opening the camera's own gallery opens Media here; leaving either side returns to live.
- Connection setup and stalled-picture recovery have bounded waits. An established feed keeps its last picture while recovering; returning from Media starts live view once.

What to test

- Tap DE-SQ to toggle; hold for presets, Custom and direction. Try LUT, grids, rotation and playback zoom. Check your custom factor survives a restart and files remain unchanged.
- Before connecting a camera, open Report a problem in portrait and landscape. Close it and confirm pairing is still available. Share Diagnostics remains in the pairing menu.
- Connect three times, then return from Media, Settings and the background. Watch preview recovery and report any freeze with the time it happened and technical details.

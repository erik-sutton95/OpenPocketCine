New and changed

- Your cameras shows connection progress and Cancel on the selected camera. Watch a feed is the eye button beside Multiview.
- Gimbal controls has Mode, Speed, and Ramp tabs, plus Motion Control in Gimbal tools. Direction Lock holds the pointing direction as the handle rotates.
- Drag scopes, LIGHTS, and ND directly to move or resize them. Scopes can sit partly under bars, reach lower, and sit underneath the joystick cluster in either orientation.

Fixes

- Pocket 3 keeps vertical 3K in FORMAT while options load and when changing frame rate.

What to test

- Reconnect a saved camera, check progress on its row, and cancel. Check Your cameras in portrait and landscape, including the eye and grid buttons.
- Switch gimbal tabs, try Direction Lock then Follow, and open Motion Control. Mode selection switches off AirPods head tracking.
- Drag and resize scopes toward each edge, then rotate the phone. Check that fixed controls remain reachable. On Pocket 3, confirm vertical 3K stays selected when changing frame rate.

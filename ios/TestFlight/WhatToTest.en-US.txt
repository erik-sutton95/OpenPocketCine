New and changed

- Motion Control loops through saved zooms and offers Restart while paused. The existing zoom chip and long-press disc stay accessible; D-Log2 blocks zoom-changing programs.
- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) works in live view, videos and photos, with lens presets and a custom 1.00–2.00× factor in 0.01 steps.
- Report a problem is available before pairing, with optional technical details and screenshots.

Fixes

- Motion Control drags more smoothly and keeps duration dials usable after moving. Help text is brighter, and loop reversals avoid false waypoint failures.
- Preview recovers after video format loss. Connection and stalled-picture recovery have bounded waits; Media return starts live view once.

What to test

- Save A/B/C at different zooms; try both loop directions, Pause/Resume and Restart. Drag the editor, adjust a duration, then close/reopen it. Check saved settings remain and actions stay fixed.
- Tap DE-SQ to toggle; hold for presets, Custom and direction. Try LUTs, guides and playback. Before pairing, open and close Report a problem.
- Connect three times, then return from Media, Settings and the background. Report any freeze with its time and technical details.

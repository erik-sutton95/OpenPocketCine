New and changed

- Opening a clip plays the camera's small preview file so the monitor stays snappy. Share and Save still use the original camera file.
- View Assist on a clip uses a monitor icon. LUT, false colour, peaking, and zebra grade the picture itself.
- Long-press LUT: DJI / Creative / Custom. DJI Auto should grade D-Log / D-Log2 with the official cube. Creative is Mono / Contrast / Warm / Cool. Exposure is −3…+3 at ½ stop.
- In Auto, the EV sheet has Face Priority. On, the drum grays out and EV follows faces to middle gray — fast for the first couple of seconds a face is in, then about one third-stop a second.
- Saved cameras remember the camera Wi-Fi on this phone, so reconnect should not re-prompt every time.
- ISO has an Auto Native ISO switch (off keeps ISO when you change D-Log / D-Log2). The SHUTTER sheet has Speed and Angle tabs; Angle runs 5.6° to 360° and is sent as the matching 1/N.

Fixes

- Turning LUT on in library playback no longer stutters. Play and scrub should feel like LUT off.
- While recording in D-Log2 the zoom chip grays out. Tap or pinch explains that color cannot change while recording — D-Log2 cannot zoom — and stays at 1×. D-Log still zooms while rolling. Idle D-Log2 still hops. The toast sits under the top bar in DISP 1 and on the picture edge in DISP 2.
- Traffic Lights crush/clip compensation starts at 0 stops. Long-press LIGHTS if you want the old ¼-stop tolerance.
- First connect no longer sits on Waiting for live-view when a few video packets arrive and then freeze.
- After leaving a clip, a dead picture with live camera status rebuilds the socket in about two seconds instead of sitting black for 15.
- The live feed comes up as soon as the camera answers. If the picture dies while the camera is still talking, the app asks for a fresh frame instead of sitting black.
- Live WAVE follows the picture instead of stepping every other frame. PARADE / HISTO / VECTOR share that tap.
- Stick and zoom stay together (zoom above the stick), not glued to record. Rotate-180 inverts stick L/R when the turn finishes; joystick-only 180 does not. Live extra-mirrors at 180 when Control Center Selfie Flip is off; Flip on skips it. A hideable debug plate shows TT180 / Flip.

What to test

- Open a 720p clip, play with LUT off, then turn on the official D-Log2 cube. Play and scrub should stay as smooth as LUT off. Auto LUT should pick D-Log vs D-Log2 from the original take (not the 720p preview). Disconnect, reopen that clip — Auto should still pick the same cube. Proxy tag on 720p-only cache. Long-press LUT, pull −1.0 / −2.0. With LUT on, tap next clip — the look should already be on the new picture.
- Share that clip with Bake LUT on. Bake exposure should be on by default — a −1.0 pull should land in the file, matching the monitor. Turn Bake exposure off to keep the cube at 0.0. Share without Bake LUT still sends the original camera clip, not a small preview.
- Leave the clip and confirm live view returns in a couple of seconds — not a long black well.
- Color D-Log2, hit REC: zoom chip should gray. Tap and pinch should toast and stay at 1×. Stop — idle D-Log2 should still hop and zoom. D-Log while rolling should still zoom. In DISP 1 the toast sits under the top bar; DISP 2 it sits on the feed edge.
- On live, turn WAVE on and pan the Pocket. Traces should move with the picture, not hold two frames. Then triple-tap 180: stick L/R should match the picture. Flip off extra-mirrors; Flip on does not. Toggle Flip several times — the debug plate Flip line should keep tracking. HIDE puts the plate away.

New and changed

- First TestFlight of OpenPocketCine. Pair over Bluetooth, join the camera's own Wi-Fi, and watch a live view with waveform, Traffic Lights, and other assists. Install this build even if an older OpenPocketCine is already on the phone — it is a new app listing.

Fixes

- Nothing to call out yet — this is the first TestFlight build. If pairing, live view, or record misbehaves, that is the report we need.

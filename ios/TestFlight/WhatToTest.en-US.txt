New and changed

- Settings now opens Support in GitHub Q&A, Report a problem on the bug form, and Feature request in Ideas.
- In-app privacy copy names optional Frame.io, the share sheet, and on-device face boxes, and points at openpocketcine.app/privacy.

Fixes

- Feature request and Q&A buttons in Settings pointed at GitHub URLs that did not match the live Discussions categories.

What to test

- From Operator Setup, tap Support, Report a problem, and Feature request and confirm the GitHub pages that open.
- Open Privacy and skim the Frame.io / share / face-box language.
- Pair an Osmo Pocket 4 or 4 Pro, join its Wi-Fi, and confirm live view fills the monitor.
- Start and stop a take from the phone, then confirm it on the camera body.

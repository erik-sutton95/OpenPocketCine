New features

- Your cameras: tap Add setup on a saved camera, enter this phone's Personal Hotspot name and password, and check the camera joins the hotspot and goes live.
- Then tap that camera's Camera Wi-Fi chip and check it returns to its own Wi-Fi and connects as before.
- Multiview's shared Wi-Fi join was reorganized internally: add two or more cameras and confirm Pocket 3, Pocket 4, Pocket 4 Pro and Nano still show preview and recover after an interruption.
- Close Multiview and check each camera returns to its own Wi-Fi.

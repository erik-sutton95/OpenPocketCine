Since open beta build 138

New and changed

- Tabs use one highlighted edge instead of boxes, lists fade softly at their edges and tool buttons share one size.
- Live View indicators are glass pills for camera battery, phone battery and link. Tap the link pill to swap signal and feed fps.
- REC setup shows your format and color, such as 4K 25p over D-Log2, in line with the timecode.
- Joystick size: Small, Medium or Large in Settings, Controls. Its ink turns dark over bright scenes and light over dark ones.
- Double-tap the same spot on the picture to start object tracking, as on the camera.
- Multiview redesign: bigger main picture, scrolling camera strip, Live View's toolbar, and camera values inside the selected tile. Starts with Auto LUT on.
- Multiview Camera settings is a side panel with a tab per camera and a live preview of that camera.
- Multiview repairs lost decoder references after returning from another app, keeping the camera connected while it waits for a picture.
- Multiview uses the Add setup Wi-Fi and Hotspot flow: current, saved and nearby networks, remembered passwords, compatibility help and retry.
- Your cameras: Add setup lets a saved camera connect over your Wi-Fi network or this iPhone's Personal Hotspot, on every Osmo camera.
- A setup connect shows a progress bar with one line of status. If it fails, choose Edit setup, Try again or Camera Wi-Fi; common router causes are named.
- The next Camera Wi-Fi connect moves the camera back onto its own network.
- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt meters from the camera's own sensor, with a bubble for top-down and straight-up shots.
- Gimbal drawer Double-tap: keep Recenter, or choose Level so a joystick or gamepad double-tap moves the lens to level or straight down. Experimental.
- Osmo Action 6 support: live view, D-Log M with the official LUT, Photo, Timelapse and a live APERTURE tile. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.
- Live monitoring with a look, peaking and waveform uses about 85% less processor power, so the iPhone runs cooler. Picture and frame rate are unchanged.

Fixes

- Shutter angle stays at your chosen value when changing frame rate. Opening the picker no longer changes the saved angle.
- Tapping to move focus no longer snaps back to the old point first. A new track no longer flashes on a previously tracked face.
- A vertical camera feed fits whole in portrait, and Live View refits when the camera turns between horizontal and vertical.
- Value drums open on the current value instead of sliding in.
- Multiview Exit retries a temporary pairing refusal before showing an error; cameras already returned to their Wi-Fi stay untouched.
- Wi-Fi headings and fields align, Connect stays above the keyboard, and connecting or cancelling waits for scan cleanup.
- Osmo Nano no longer shows zoom controls, and DISP settings list Zoom Chip and Gimbal Stick only when the camera has them.
- The REC and scan pulses no longer hold the display at its highest refresh rate, and paused clips stop redrawing.
- Live View no longer freezes for several seconds after a single dropped video packet.
- Switching clips in Media no longer freezes the app while the previous clip is still being processed.

What to test

- In Multiview, try Grid and Center stage in both orientations, open Camera settings, and switch apps to check feeds resume. Exit once.
- Double-tap an object to track it, with a face in the scene. Set the joystick to Large and check its ink over bright and dark scenes.
- Turn the camera vertical in portrait: the whole picture should show. Set 180° and change FPS: angle should stay 180°.
- Try Wi-Fi and Hotspot in Add setup and Multiview, type a password in both orientations, cancel a scan, then reconnect.
- Turn on LEVEL and roll the handle in FPV. On Nano, confirm there is no zoom chip. If you have an Action 6, check live view and APERTURE.

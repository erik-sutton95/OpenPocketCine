New and changed

- Motion Control adds a fixed Zoom slider for setting points, loops back and forth between saved zoom amounts, and offers Restart while paused. D-Log2 blocks zoom-changing programs.
- Pocket 3 zoom stops follow FORMAT, with a note when the ceiling changes. Refused zoom values settle back to the camera's reading.
- HDR display brightens the phone panel; off by default. Screen recording and AirPlay use normal SDR.

Fixes

- The zoom dial retains fractional input without snapping to hundredths or whole zoom stops. Crossing a whole stop gives one haptic pulse; camera lens smoothness still needs testing.
- Exact loop reversals accept confirmed arrival with native motor easing, preventing a false waypoint-verification error.
- Motion Control waypoint letters and path now compensate for selfie orientation and MIRROR. Saved positions and motion stay unchanged.
- Connection and picture recovery keep the last picture and use bounded retries. Media return starts live view once.

What to test

- Use Zoom without closing Motion Control; SET A/B/C at different amounts. Test both loop directions and Pause/Resume. Compare slow dial zoom with Mimo. D-Log2 recording must disable the slider.
- Pause and choose Restart: the saved program starts again from A. Scroll in both orientations: actions stay fixed. Close/reopen retains the program; Clear removes points.
- Connect repeatedly, use controls and open Media, then return. Check Pocket 3 zoom limits per FORMAT and HDR display while recording the phone screen.

New and changed

- On Osmo Nano, zoom is hidden: no zoom chip, pinch zoom, hold disc or controller zoom. Pocket cameras zoom as before.
- DISP settings list Zoom Chip and Gimbal Stick only when the connected camera has them.

Fixes

- Multiview preview recovery and camera support were reorganized internally; iPhone and iPad behavior should not change.

What to test

- Connect a Nano: check there is no zoom chip, pinch does not zoom, and controller L1/R1 and L2/R2 do nothing.
- Switch to a Pocket without relaunching and check the zoom chip and gimbal stick come back.
- Add two or more cameras in Multiview and confirm Pocket 3, Pocket 4, Pocket 4 Pro and Nano still show preview.

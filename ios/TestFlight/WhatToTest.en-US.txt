Since open beta build 63

New and changed

- UI 2.0: Sora/Lucide, consistent buttons, Media footer, pull-refresh and hold/sideways selection. Pocket 4 Pro: tap 1×/3×, double tap 6×/12×; hold for zoom dial.
- Tap camera values for full drawers; hold or drag for compact dials. FORMAT, COLOR and mode hang from the top; landscape has no extra category tabs.
- Gimbal Mode, Speed and Ramp have tabs. Experimental Motion Control adds timed A/B/C moves, smoothing, countdown, Pause, Resume and Stop.
- Direction Lock holds the camera's direction as its body rotates. Select another mode to release it.
- Experimental AirPods head tracking follows calibrated head direction. Manual input wins; changing gimbal mode turns tracking off.
- Scopes, LIGHTS and ND open centered; drag and resize them. AUDIO adds movable meters and channel dB readings. Saved placements stay yours.
- ND suggests Stops, ND factor or optical density. False color adds CineStop, six-zone IRE and EL Zone scales.
- Multiview asks for network/password each session. Larger 2×2 tiles; WI-FI below FIT/FILL. Experimental.
- Share this feed lets iPhones/iPads on camera Wi-Fi watch with local assists, request controls and reconnect. Hold Multiview on Your cameras to browse feeds.
- Apple Watch shows preview, timecode, storage, battery and shutter controls. The paired iPhone stays connected.
- Convert log on Share matches D-Log/D-Log2, separately from Bake LUT. Originals stay untouched; D-Log M conversion is excluded.
- Automatic reports ask once after install/update with no saved choice. Enable or Not now is remembered. Report a problem is separate.

Fixes

- Capture tabs handle mode changes; proxy clips stream to disk.
- Playback assists wait for the clip to be ready before requesting its first frame.
- Upscaler preparation leaves interface controls responsive while it starts.
- Media cache checks and deletion run in the background. Clear keeps clip/color details; failed deletion can be retried.
- iOS 17 no longer crashes when Live View releases AirPods head tracking.
- Lock matches Settings/Media; battery uses %. Portrait centers with STBY/timecode/REC SETUP below the cutout; assists, FIT/FILL and joystick stay above camera values.
- Blurred panels clear corners; shadows are tighter and audio meters slim. Signal colors match Settings. View Assist previews work with scopes off.
- Zebra Highlight uses the hottest color channel and defaults to 99 IRE. Numeric options stay above the keyboard, with Done to dismiss it.
- Pocket 3 COLOR maps Normal, HDR/HLG and D-Log M. FORMAT is 16:9 and 1:1; 9:16 is body Lock Portrait unless reported. Shutter and 1:1 guides apply.
- Normal-color Auto ISO starts at 50 on Pocket 3/Pocket 4, and 100 on Pocket 4 Pro.
- Nano COLOR matches Normal 8-bit, Normal 10-bit and D-Log M. Live view handles larger Nano frames more reliably.
- D-Log M scopes use their own scale. Scene-stop values are estimates; sensor clipping limits are uncalibrated.
- Renamed camera Wi-Fi uses its current name. Failed joins clear stale details; setup warns when a VPN/ad blocker may block live view.
- Recovery retains the last picture around zoom, settings, LUT 50/50 and clip playback. False color stays visible through Auto ISO changes.

- Decline reporting and relaunch. Rotate drawers; tap versus hold dials. Check zoom, Media selection, playback and numeric options with the keyboard.
- On Pocket 3, check color, 2.7K, 1:1, shutter Speed/Angle, 16:9 guides, recording and reconnect. 9:16 is body Lock Portrait.
- Try joystick, Motion Control, Direction Lock, false-color scales and ND. Keep live view running while changing settings and reopening clips.
- Check Multiview network, WI-FI and FIT/FILL; on iOS 17, enter/exit Live View. Try sharing, Watch and Convert log. Multiview, Motion Control and head tracking remain experimental.

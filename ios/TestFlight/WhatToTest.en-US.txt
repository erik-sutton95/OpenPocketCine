New and changed

- A connected game controller's left stick drives the gimbal; small throws crawl, full throw is fastest. Cross/A records. Circle/B recenters. Square/X is rotate-180. Triangle/Y tracks a face or cancels. L1/R1 jump zoom, L2/R2 hold-to-zoom, D-pad ISO and shutter. A gimbal stop taps once.
- Head tracking is experimental: Operator Setup → Controls, Head Tracking (Experimental). Wear AirPods with motion, go live, look forward, hold still, tap Calibrate Head Lock. A 53° head turn pans the Pocket 53°. Nod is tilt. Roll is numbers only. STOP clears the lock. Off keeps AirPods as audio only.
- The on-screen gimbal stick uses the same gradual speed.
- Pair over Bluetooth, join camera Wi-Fi, watch live view. Record, ISO, white balance, and zoom from the phone.
- Share Diagnostics is on first pair and in Operator Setup → System. A TestFlight screenshot copies a short paste for feedback. No name or location.

Fixes

- In D-Log2, zoom waits for the hop to D-Log before the lens moves; the chip stays at 1× until color switches. Zoom keeps the live picture. On Pocket 3, COLOR Normal, HDR, and D-Log M match the camera. Live view comes up a couple of seconds after Wi-Fi joins. Pause VPNs or ad blockers, or exclude this app, if it never starts.
- Live view stays up while you pan, tilt, or head-track. Record, FORMAT, COLOR, white balance, or a tracking box must not freeze the picture. If it dies, the last frame stays under Reconnecting and live view returns without closing the app.
- Head tracking no longer overshoots and swings back. Turn, then nod: tilt is straight up and down. Rapid look-pause-look no longer freezes the picture.
- Power-cycling the camera mid-session keeps the last picture under Reconnecting instead of going black. A renamed Pocket Wi-Fi joins without a re-pair.
- With LUT, waveform, peaking, or Face AF on, zooming or the D-Log2 to D-Log hop no longer freeze the picture. LUT 50/50 must not drop live view or stick on Reconnecting.
- Clips or Operator Setup over live view, then back, keeps the picture moving, not a black well. On Pocket 3, a take you just recorded should list and play.
- On Pocket 3 and Pocket 4, Auto ISO ranges start at 50, matching the camera; Pocket 4 Pro starts at 100.
- Zebra: Highlight paints when any color channel runs hot. Factory Highlight is now 99 IRE; saved thresholds are unchanged. Long-press ZEBRA, tap Highlight or Midtone: the number field stays above the keyboard.

If Reconnecting appears, last picture stays and live view returns without closing the app. Pause VPNs if live view never starts.
- Connect a DualSense or any extended gamepad: left stick gimbal, Cross record, Circle recenter, Square 180, Triangle track, L1/R1 jump zoom, L2/R2 hold-to-zoom, D-pad ISO and shutter. Push into a stop: one buzz, not a rumble. Unplug rests the stick and toasts.

- Tap Share Diagnostics before pairing finishes. After live: record a short take, open it in the library with LUT on. Share sends the original file.

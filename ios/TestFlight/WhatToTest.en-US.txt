New and changed

- Your cameras: Add setup lets a saved camera connect over your Wi-Fi or this iPhone's Hotspot, on every Osmo camera. Camera Wi-Fi stays as the other setup.
- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt meters from the camera's own sensor, plus Double-tap → Level in the gimbal drawer to move the lens to level or straight down.
- Osmo Action 6 support with live view, D-Log M and an APERTURE control (not yet checked on a real Action 6). On Osmo Nano, zoom controls are hidden.

Fixes

- Live video no longer stops shortly after connecting while the app is busy.
- In Auto exposure, SHUTTER shows the shutter the camera actually applied.
- Live monitoring with looks, scopes and glass panels uses much less power and runs cooler.

What to test

- Turn on LEVEL and roll the handle in FPV: the roll meter should move with the picture. With Double-tap set to Level, angle the handle forward and double-tap.
- Monitor for 10 minutes with LUT, PEAK and WAVE on, then record: glass panels and the REC pulse should look as before, and the phone should stay cooler.
- Try Add setup › Wi-Fi and › Hotspot, then Camera Wi-Fi again. Check Nano has no zoom chip, and Action 6 live view, APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.

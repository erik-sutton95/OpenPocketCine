New and changed

- In Auto, the EV sheet has Face Priority. On, the drum grays out and EV follows faces to middle gray, one third-stop every couple of seconds.
- The EV tile keeps the number and puts a small face mark on the EV label.

Fixes

- Nothing to call out yet — this build adds Face Priority on Auto EV.

What to test

- Set MODE to Auto, open EV, turn Face Priority on, and confirm the drum is gray and the EV tile shows a face mark.
- Put a face in frame (then two). EV should move without touching the drum; two faces should sit between the two skin tones.
- Cover every face — EV should hold, not snap to 0.0.
- Switch to Manual — the SHUTTER tile is speed/angle again, no face mark.

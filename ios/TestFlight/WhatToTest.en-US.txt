New and changed

- A connected game controller's left stick drives the gimbal. Small throws crawl; full throw is fastest. Cross/A records. Circle/B recenters. Square/X is rotate-180. Triangle/Y tracks a face, or cancels tracking. L1/R1 jump zoom out/in. L2/R2 hold-to-zoom (press harder for faster). D-pad up/down ISO, left/right shutter. Connect and unplug should toast Gamepad connected / disconnected and rest the stick. Operator Setup → Controls → Gamepad shows Connected while the pad is on. A gimbal stop taps once after the head moves then stalls (Haptics off is silent).
- Head tracking is experimental: Operator Setup → Controls, turn Head Tracking (Experimental) on. Wear AirPods with motion, go live, look forward, hold still, tap Calibrate Head Lock (centered above the bottom bars). That pose is shared zero — a 53° head turn should pan the Pocket 53° (well shows head vs body vs err). Nod is tilt. Roll is numbers only. STOP clears the lock. Off keeps AirPods as audio only. Allow Motion & Fitness if iOS asks.
- The on-screen gimbal stick uses the same gradual speed.
- Pair over Bluetooth, join the camera's Wi-Fi, and watch a live view. Record, ISO, white balance, and zoom from the phone.

Fixes

- In D-Log2, zoom waits for the hop to D-Log before the lens moves. The chip stays at 1× until color actually switches, then zoom follows. Live view should still come up in a couple of seconds after Wi-Fi joins, not sit on Waiting for live view.
- Zoom must keep the live picture, not just HUD and stick. COLOR still lists what this camera has: D-Log2 only on Pocket 4 Pro.

What to test

- Pair a Pocket, go live, and pan with the on-screen stick: half throw should be slower than a full push.
- Connect a DualSense (or any extended gamepad). Left stick should move the gimbal. Cross records. Circle recenters. Square does 180. Triangle tracks a face. L1/R1 jump zoom. L2/R2 hold-to-zoom. D-pad ISO and shutter. Push into a tilt or pan stop — one buzz, not a continuous rumble. Haptics off should be silent. Unplug should rest the stick and toast disconnected.
- On Pocket 4 Pro in D-Log2 (idle, not recording): press R2 or pinch. COLOR should switch to D-Log first, then the picture should zoom. The chip must not run ahead of the lens.
- Record a short take, then open it in the library with LUT on. Share should send the original file, not a small preview.

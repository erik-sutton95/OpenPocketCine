New and changed

- UI 2.0: Sora/Lucide, consistent buttons, Media footer, pull-refresh and hold/sideways selection. Pocket 4 Pro zoom: tap 1×/3×, double tap 6×/12×; hold for dial.
- Gimbal Mode, Speed and Ramp each have a tab in the wider panel. Motion Control remains experimental, with timed A/B/C moves, smoothing, countdown, Pause, Resume and Stop.
- Fresh scopes, LIGHTS and ND open centered; drag to place and use a corner to resize. Saved placements stay yours.

Fixes

- iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 no longer crash at launch. Experimental AirPods head tracking stays available on iOS 18 and later.
- Live-view stability improves around zoom, settings, LUT 50/50 and clip playback. Recovery retains the last picture.

What to test

- On an iPhone or iPad running iOS 17, launch the app, open Live View and return to Your cameras. Confirm the app stays open.
- Compare panel blur over bright/dark footage. Rotate pages and drawers; tap versus hold drums; open FORMAT in both orientations.
- Keep a live session running while changing settings, zooming and reopening clips. Share diagnostics with the time of any freeze or stutter.

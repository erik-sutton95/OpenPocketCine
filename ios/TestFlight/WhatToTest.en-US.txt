Since open beta build 158

New and changed

- New CineStop false color: greens below 18% gray, gray at 18%, pink +1 skin, yellow +2 upper skin, red clip. Old scale is now Video; Read picks 709 or LOG.
- Long-press MIRROR for Horizontal and Vertical. Turn both on to see an underslung camera, such as on a car mount, the right way up.
- Long-press the picture to lock exposure: the focus box turns yellow with AE-L. The sun-and-lock key beside Recenter unlocks. WB Mode adds AWB Lock.
- Multiview redesign: bigger main picture, scrolling camera strip, Live View's toolbar, and camera values inline in the selected tile.
- Multiview Grid and Center stage both work in portrait and landscape, with Exit and Wi-Fi together and Record and DISP in Live View's places.
- Multiview Camera settings is a side panel with a tab per camera and a live preview of that camera.
- Multiview starts with Auto LUT on, shows HH:MM:SS timecode and uses Live View battery pills. Camera options open in a compact menu.
- Multiview uses the Add setup Wi-Fi and Hotspot flow: current, saved and nearby networks, remembered passwords, compatibility help and retry.
- Double-tap the same spot on the picture to start object tracking, as on the camera.
- Joystick size: Small, Medium or Large in Settings, Controls. Its ink turns dark over bright scenes and light over dark ones.
- Live View indicators are glass pills for camera battery, phone battery and link. Tap the link pill to swap signal and feed fps.
- REC setup shows your format and color, such as 4K 25p over D-Log2, in line with the timecode.
- Tabs use one highlighted edge instead of boxes, lists fade softly at their edges and tool buttons share one size.

Fixes

- Traffic Lights no longer flag clipping just under clip, and zebra at 100% now marks clipped highlights.
- Pairing says what blocks it: Bluetooth or Local Network access (with Open Settings), Personal Hotspot, or DJI Frame Tap and DJI Mimo holding the camera.
- The gimbal joystick moves as fast on diagonals as straight up, down or sideways. AE lock gives a stronger double tap.
- Media lists complete SD and internal libraries, retries Nano's briefly empty first response and keeps capacity visible during playback.
- Switching clips in Media no longer freezes the app while the previous clip is still being processed.
- Live View no longer freezes for several seconds after a single dropped video packet. The picture returns with the camera's next keyframe.
- Shutter angle stays at your chosen value when changing frame rate. Opening the picker no longer changes the saved angle.
- Tapping to move focus no longer snaps back to the old point first. A new track no longer flashes on a previously tracked face.
- A vertical camera feed fits whole in portrait, and Live View refits when the camera turns between horizontal and vertical.
- Value drums open on the current value instead of sliding in.
- Multiview repairs its picture after returning from another app, keeping the camera connected while it waits.
- Multiview Exit retries a temporary pairing refusal before showing an error; cameras already returned to their Wi-Fi stay untouched.
- Wi-Fi headings and fields align, Connect stays above the keyboard, and connecting or cancelling waits for scan cleanup.

What to test

- Open Media on a camera with many clips or both storage types. On Nano, open it right after playback starts. Confirm all clips and capacity appear.
- In Multiview, try Grid and Center stage in both orientations, Camera settings, an app switch, Wi-Fi and Hotspot, then Exit once.
- Double-tap an object to track it, with a face in the scene. Set the joystick to Large and check its ink over bright and dark scenes.
- Turn the camera vertical in portrait: the whole picture should show. Set 180° and change FPS: angle should stay 180°.
- Pair with Local Network or Bluetooth off for OpenPocketCine, or Personal Hotspot on: the error should name the fix.

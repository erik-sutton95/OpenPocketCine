New features

- Your cameras: tap Add setup on a saved camera and choose Wi-Fi or Hotspot. Wi-Fi should list nearby networks by itself within about 20 seconds; Hotspot should fill in this iPhone's name. Check the camera goes live on that network.
- While connecting, check the progress bar and its status line read well. If a connect fails, try Edit setup, Try again and Connect over Camera Wi-Fi instead.
- Afterwards tap the Camera Wi-Fi chip and check the camera returns to its own Wi-Fi. Action 6 owners: please try Add setup too.
- Multiview's Wi-Fi join was reorganized internally: add two or more cameras and confirm preview still works and recovers, and that closing Multiview returns each camera to its own Wi-Fi.

New and changed

- Share this feed in Operator Setup → Sharing sends the picture to OpenPocketCine iPhones and iPads on the same camera Wi-Fi. Show Wi-Fi code on the host, scan it with Camera on the watcher, accept Join Network, then return to Watch a feed. Only the host pairs with the camera in the app.
- Watchers have local assists and scopes, camera readings, REC tally, and Clean view. Request control to record, focus, or change supported ISO, shutter, and zoom settings. Release gives control back.
- Broadcast priority sets the quality ceiling. Sharing can lower its bitrate when the host picture slows.
- FALSE Scale adds CineStop video stripes, EL Zone scene stops (+6 white / −6 black), and six-zone IRE over gray.
- Tap ND for a suggested screw-on filter and stop count. It starts off; the app cannot set a filter.
- Game controllers drive the gimbal, recording, zoom, tracking, and exposure. Experimental AirPods head tracking is in Controls; calibrate while still and live. STOP clears the lock.

Fixes

- Interrupted watcher feeds keep the last picture and retry three times. A stopped feed leaves its error and Choose a feed visible instead of opening camera pairing.
- Passcode refusals arrive before the connection closes, so the watcher can enter the correct code.
- A slow watcher waits for a fresh keyframe while other watchers continue. Local assists and mirror stay selected as frames arrive.
- The camera picture stays through settings and clips, zoom, exposure changes, and recovery. A frozen picture with live timecode is a bug. Pause VPNs or ad blockers if live view never starts.

What to test

- Start a watcher on different Wi-Fi. Follow Show Wi-Fi code and join the camera Wi-Fi before finding the host. Try a wrong watcher passcode, then the correct one.
- Note host FPS with sharing off. Turn sharing on, join one watcher, then two or more. Pan and record for five minutes with LUT and waveform on. Check host and watchers for stutter or growing delay.
- Move one watcher out of range, then return it. The host and other watchers should keep moving; the returning picture should reconnect. Leave during retries to cancel them.
- Stop and restart sharing. Leave and rejoin. Request control, grant it, test focus at the picture edges with mirror on and off, then reclaim control on the host. The watcher must stop sending camera commands after losing control.
- Try Clean view and assists in portrait and landscape. Long-press FALSE and compare CineStop, EL Zone (mid-gray gray, hot sky white), and IRE. Tap ND on a bright take and check its filter/stop reading changes with the picture. Report device models, watcher count, and which picture slowed first. Multiple-watcher capacity still needs device testing.

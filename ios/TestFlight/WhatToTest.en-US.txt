New and changed

- Gimbal → Track adds experimental phone-side tracking. Tune sensitivity, Smoothness, dead band, Lerp, Motion matching, framing and motion limits. Settings last for this session.
- Pocket 3 zoom stops follow FORMAT. HDR display optionally brightens the phone panel; recordings and camera HDR/HLG are unchanged.

Fixes

- Live view keeps its last picture during recovery. Connection setup waits for the camera command window, and repeated controls cannot delay stalled-picture recovery indefinitely.
- Phone tracking matches subject velocity and removes a speed bottleneck from delayed attitude. Diverging camera feedback ends tracking.

What to test

- In Gimbal → Track, use Responsive, Motion matching 100% and maximum speed 90°/s. Follow a walking person, stop and reverse. Compare Gentle/Balanced and front/selfie views.
- Tap a face and briefly turn away: check recovery and smooth motion. Cover it for over a second or cross two faces: select again. Manual controls, zoom and leaving the app must stop tracking.
- Run tracking for five minutes with LUT/WAVE. Compare feed smoothness and phone temperature with tracking off. Share diagnostics and the time of any freeze or unexpected movement.

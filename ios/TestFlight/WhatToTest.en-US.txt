New and changed

- Live monitoring with looks, scopes and glass panels uses much less power and runs cooler: glass blur now runs on the GPU, and the REC pulse and scan dots no longer hold the screen at 120 Hz.
- On Osmo Nano, zoom is hidden: no zoom chip, pinch zoom, hold disc or controller zoom. DISP settings list Zoom Chip and Gimbal Stick only when the camera has them.
- Osmo Action 6 support: live view, Normal 10-bit and D-Log M color, the official D-Log M LUT, and an APERTURE control in place of FOCUS. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.

Fixes

- Live video no longer stops shortly after connecting while the app is busy.
- In Auto exposure, SHUTTER shows the shutter the camera actually applied.

What to test

- Monitor for 10 minutes with LUT, PEAK and WAVE on, then record a take: glass panels, the REC pulse and face tracking should look as before, and the phone should stay cooler.
- Connect a Nano: check there is no zoom chip and that pinch and controller L1/R1 and L2/R2 do not zoom. Switch to a Pocket without relaunching and check zoom and the gimbal stick return.
- If you have an Action 6: pair it, confirm live view appears, then try APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.

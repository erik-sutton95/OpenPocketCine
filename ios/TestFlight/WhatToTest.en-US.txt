New and changed

- Experimental Multiview: tap the grid icon in Your cameras. Put supported cameras on one Wi-Fi network and monitor up to four from this device, with independent LUTs, settings readouts and reported timecode where supported.
- Center stage and adaptive grid layouts include portrait Fit/Fill, per-camera recording and Record all/Stop all. Recording tiles show a red border. Double-tap a feed for Live View controls; the grid icon returns to the stage.
- Multiview remembers cameras and layout. Network passwords are saved securely on this device. Personal Hotspot is experimental and must be enabled in Settings.
- Pocket 3 FORMAT now offers its documented normal-video resolutions, including 2.7K, when the camera does not supply a format list.

Fixes

- Pocket 3 first-picture startup and Nano short preview freezes have decoder and frame-assembly fixes.
- False color keeps its paint during exposure changes. False color, peaking and zebras resize with the picture during rotation and Fit/Fill changes.
- D-Log M scopes use the preview signal directly instead of D-Log limits. DLM approximate stop readings are estimates, not measured sensor clipping limits.
- Multiview checks for new pictures after returning from another app, including with LUT on. Pocket 3 recovery can still take about a minute.

What to test

- Pocket 3: connect repeatedly, choose 2.7K at 25p in normal Video, confirm the camera setting, record a short clip, then reconnect. Check the preview returns and the selected format remains correct.
- Nano: watch for short freezes with LUT off and on, then start and stop recording. Compare smoothness with your previous build.
- Multiview: add Pocket 3, Pocket 4 Pro and Nano on shared Wi-Fi. Try Record all/Stop all, individual recording, tile borders, LUTs and both layouts. Rotate and toggle Fit/Fill; controls and overlays must stay aligned.
- Switch to another app briefly and return. Test Reconnect on a failed tile. Close and reopen Multiview to check saved cameras and network restoration. Scan-only cancellation has a known cleanup gap: if the camera's own Wi-Fi stays unavailable, restart the camera.
- In D-Log M, enable waveform and false color while exposure changes. Report flashing paint, mismatched overlays or frozen pictures using Share Diagnostics. Hotspot, setup cancellation and restoration remain experimental.

New and changed

- Anamorphic Desqueeze (DE-SQ) works in live view, video playback and photos. Choose a lens preset or a custom 1.00–2.00× factor in 0.01 steps.
- Pocket 3 zoom follows FORMAT limits. HDR display brightens the phone panel; screen recording and AirPlay stay SDR.

Fixes

- Connection recovery keeps the last picture and responds sooner to lost video. Repeated controls cannot delay recovery indefinitely.
- Returning from Media starts live view once and keeps the current picture host through resizing.

What to test

- Tap DE-SQ to toggle; hold for presets, Custom and direction. Try LUT, grids, rotation and playback zoom. Check your custom factor survives a restart and files remain unchanged.
- Connect three times, then run live view for five minutes. Open Media, play a clip and return repeatedly; report any freeze and its time.
- On Pocket 3, check 1080 zooms to 4×, 2.7K to 3× and 4K to 2×. With HDR display on, check the feed stays upright and screen recordings remain SDR.

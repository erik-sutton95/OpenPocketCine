New and changed

- Pocket 3 zoom stops follow FORMAT, with a note when the ceiling changes. Refused zoom values settle back to the camera's reading.
- HDR display brightens the phone panel; off by default. Screen recording and AirPlay use normal SDR. Camera HDR/HLG recording is unchanged.

Fixes

- An established feed keeps its last picture instead of returning to Waiting for live view when frame statistics age out.
- Connection setup waits for the camera's command window. Recovery can act sooner after lost video data while keeping the last picture.
- Repeated controls cannot indefinitely delay stalled-picture recovery. Multiview and resizing keep the current picture host.
- Media return starts live view once. Older picture-repair deadlines no longer reconnect just because you opened Media.

What to test

- Connect and disconnect three times, then run for five minutes with LUT/WAVE and normal controls. Share diagnostics and the time of slow startup or any freeze.
- Open Media, play a clip and return repeatedly. Cover Settings, rotate and switch single-camera/Multiview views; confirm picture returns.
- On Pocket 3, check 1080 zooms to 4×, 2.7K to 3× and 4K to 2×. With HDR display on, check the feed stays upright and screen recordings remain normal SDR.

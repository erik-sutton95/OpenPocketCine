This is the cumulative update for testers moving from **open beta build 138
(0.1.0) to 0.1.5**. Android notes cover the same release window; its build
number differs.

Osmo Action 6 support, LEVEL and Double-tap Level arrive on both platforms.
iOS adds per-camera Wi-Fi and Hotspot setups; Android adds Multiview. Multiview,
Double-tap Level and Action 6 remain experimental.

## iOS

### iOS: new and changed

- Tabs use one highlighted bottom/left edge, without boxes. Scrolling content fades softly at edges with more to scroll.
- Multiview Camera settings opens a popup with one tab per camera and the same controls as Live View.
- Multiview starts with Auto LUT on, shows HH:MM:SS timecode and uses Live View battery gauges. Camera options open in a compact floating menu.
- Multiview repairs lost decoder references after returning from another app, retaining the camera connection while waiting for a new picture.
- Center stage enlarges the main picture beside a scrolling camera strip, with Wi-Fi below Exit and collapsible tools. Grid fills the space; DISP and Record keep Live View positions.
- Multiview uses the Add setup Wi-Fi and Hotspot flow: current, saved and nearby networks, remembered passwords, compatibility help and retry without leaving the password screen.
- Your cameras: Add setup lets a saved camera connect over your Wi-Fi network or this iPhone's Personal Hotspot, on every Osmo camera. Camera Wi-Fi stays as the other setup.
- A setup connect shows a progress bar with one line of status. If it fails, choose Edit setup, Try again or Camera Wi-Fi; common router causes are named.
- The next Camera Wi-Fi connect moves the camera back onto its own network.
- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt meters from the camera's own sensor, with a bubble for top-down and straight-up shots. Shows No level data instead of a false level.
- Gimbal drawer Double-tap: keep Recenter, or choose Level so a joystick or gamepad double-tap moves the lens to level or straight down. Experimental.
- Osmo Action 6 support: live view, Normal 10-bit and D-Log M color with the official LUT, Photo and Timelapse. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.
- On Action 6, an APERTURE tile takes the FOCUS slot with a live iris readout. Gimbal and focus controls are hidden.
- Live monitoring with a look, peaking and waveform uses about 85% less processor power, and about 70% less while recording, so the iPhone runs cooler. Picture and frame rate are unchanged.

### iOS: fixes

- Shutter angle stays at your chosen value when changing frame rate; shutter time follows after camera confirmation. Opening the picker no longer changes the saved angle.
- Multiview Exit retries a temporary pairing refusal before showing an error; cameras already returned to their Wi-Fi stay untouched.
- Wi-Fi headings and fields align, Connect stays at the bottom above the keyboard, and connecting/cancelling waits for scan cleanup.
- The joystick adapts between bright and dark ink over the picture, without a dark halo or image sampling.
- Osmo Nano no longer shows zoom controls: the zoom chip, pinch, hold disc and gamepad zoom are hidden, and no zoom is sent.
- DISP settings list Zoom Chip and Gimbal Stick only when the connected camera has them.
- The REC and scan pulses no longer hold the display at its highest refresh rate, and paused clips stop redrawing.
- Live View no longer freezes for several seconds after a single dropped video packet. The picture returns as soon as the camera sends a fresh keyframe.
- Switching clips in Media no longer freezes the app while the previous clip is still being processed.

### iOS: what to test

- In Multiview, rotate and scroll the camera strip. Set 180° and change FPS: angle should stay 180°. Switch apps, check feeds resume, then Exit once.
- Try Wi-Fi and Hotspot in Add setup and Multiview. Check current/saved/nearby networks, type a password in both orientations, cancel a scan, then reconnect.
- Turn on LEVEL and roll the handle in FPV: the roll meter should move with the picture. With Double-tap set to Level, angle the handle forward and double-tap.
- Monitor for 10 minutes with LUT, PEAK and WAVE on, then record: glass panels and the REC pulse should look as before, and the phone should stay cooler.
- On Nano, confirm there is no zoom chip. If you have an Action 6, check live view, APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.

## Android

### Android: new and changed

- Tabs use one highlighted bottom/left edge, without boxes. Scrolling content fades softly at edges with more to scroll.
- Multiview Camera settings opens a popup with one tab per camera and the same controls as Live View.
- Multiview starts with Auto LUT on, shows HH:MM:SS timecode and uses Live View battery gauges. Camera options open in a compact floating menu.
- Multiview network setup groups current, saved and nearby Wi-Fi, scans automatically with a camera, remembers passwords and offers hotspot help and retry on the password screen.
- Experimental Multiview: the grid button on Your cameras monitors up to four Osmo cameras on shared Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot, with Auto LUT and per-tile or group recording.
- Center stage enlarges the main picture beside a scrolling camera strip, with Wi-Fi below Exit and collapsible tools. Grid fills the space; DISP and Record keep Live View positions.
- New LEVEL View Assist: roll and tilt meters from the camera's own sensor, with a bubble for top-down and straight-up shots. Shows No level data instead of a false level.
- Gimbal drawer Double-tap: keep Recenter, or choose Level so a joystick or gamepad double-tap moves the lens to level or straight down. Experimental.
- Osmo Action 6 support: live view, Normal 10-bit and D-Log M color with the official LUT, Photo and Timelapse. Not yet checked on a real Action 6.
- On Action 6, an APERTURE tile takes the FOCUS slot with a live iris readout. Gimbal and focus controls are hidden.
- Live View uses about 65% less CPU with a LUT and 60% less with PEAK and WAVE on a Galaxy S25, and runs cooler. Frame rate and picture are unchanged.
- Devices without Google Play can install the sideload APK from the project's GitHub Releases page.

### Android: fixes

- Shutter angle stays at your chosen value when changing frame rate; shutter time follows after camera confirmation. Opening the picker no longer changes the saved angle.
- Multiview Exit retries a temporary pairing refusal before showing an error; cameras already returned to their Wi-Fi stay untouched.
- Wi-Fi headings and fields align, Connect stays at the bottom above the keyboard, and connecting/cancelling waits for scan cleanup.
- The joystick adapts between bright and dark ink over the picture, without a dark halo or image sampling.
- Deleting a clip from clip playback works again: Delete and Cancel no longer play or pause the clip behind the dialog.
- Osmo Nano no longer shows zoom controls: the zoom chip, pinch, hold disc and gamepad zoom are hidden, and no zoom is sent.
- DISP settings list Zoom Chip and Gimbal Stick only when the connected camera has them.
- Live View no longer crashes on some phone and monitor screen sizes while gimbal controls are shown.
- Live View no longer freezes for several seconds after a single dropped video packet. The picture returns as soon as the camera sends a fresh keyframe.
- Live View no longer crashes over and over on some MediaTek phones. After two crashes it uses a compatible display mode for this version.

### Android: what to test

- If Live View freezes while the camera stays connected, note the time and send Connection setup → Share Diagnostics: it now includes more detail about the stall.
- In Multiview, rotate and scroll the camera strip. Set 180° and change FPS: angle should stay 180°. Switch apps, check feeds resume, then Exit once.
- Turn on LEVEL and roll the handle in FPV: the roll meter should move with the picture. With Double-tap set to Level, angle the handle forward and double-tap.
- In Media, try Cancel and Delete on a clip. Over bright and dark scenes, check the joystick stays visible without a halo.
- On Nano, confirm there is no zoom chip. If you have an Action 6, check live view, APERTURE, COLOR, Photo and Timelapse.

## Availability and remaining limits

- Per-camera Wi-Fi and Hotspot setups are iOS only in this release. Wi-Fi and
  Camera Wi-Fi are checked on a Pocket 4 Pro; Hotspot is still pending.
- Action 6 support is built from a capture of the camera and has not been
  checked on a physical Action 6. See the [Action 6 page](../../devices/action-6/)
  for what is and is not wired.
- Gimbal, zoom and focus controls stay off for Action, 360, drone and unknown
  cameras until they are verified.
- A sideload APK and a Google Play install are signed differently and cannot
  update each other. Remove one before installing the other.

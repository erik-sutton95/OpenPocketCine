title: OpenPocketCine 0.1.8 release notes
description: Cumulative iOS and Android changes for testers upgrading from open beta build 128 (0.1.5).
---

This public beta is the cumulative update for testers moving from **open beta build 128 (0.1.5)
to 0.1.8**. iOS and Android cover the same release window; their store build
numbers differ. It is a new version, so the first external TestFlight build of
0.1.8 goes through TestFlight Beta App Review before testers can install it.

## iOS

### iOS: new and changed

- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media controls look and behave like the other OpenCine apps.
- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting, Select all and transfer progress stay responsive while clips cache.
- Media grid is staggered: portrait photos stand upright and every item fills the space above it.
- Caching and downloads show a live speed beside the percentage, for example 18% and 42 MB/s.
- Selecting clips shows an Exit selection close beside the count, then All, Share and Delete.
- Pinch-zoomed playback now fills the screen past the clip's letterbox, up to the screen edge.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting: the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tap the storage readout to step through free space, free percentage and remaining time, the same in portrait and landscape.
- In landscape, colour sits beside the codec, and in Photo mode white balance and focus move to the top corner so captions fit.
- The open tools panel keeps the camera values visible beside it instead of hiding them.
- Portrait readouts use a three-column grid. Offline, Media shows the clips cached on this phone; an empty filter says No matching clips.
- Apple Watch can record and capture while the phone is locked. A watcher on an older app version is asked to update.
- Bluetooth Remote Shutter is now off until you turn it on in Operator Setup > Controls, and it ignores late volume changes.
- Diagnostics separate video, decoding and display activity to help investigate picture interruptions.

### iOS: fixes

- Connecting no longer freezes the app on Starting live view or Opening the video link.
- Camera Wi-Fi: an approved join is kept for rejoins, a refused join offers guidance and Wi-Fi settings, and forgetting a setup removes its network.
- The Local Network prompt comes during pairing, before the camera Wi-Fi join, and Allow carries on without relaunching the app.
- Reconnecting waits in the background, so the picture returns without Retry. The card names its 3-minute window; a dropped link shows no bars.
- Returning from Media, even after a long visit, restores the live picture instead of disconnecting the camera.
- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual and frame-rate changes; the list is no longer stuck at 1/60, and a Pocket 3 with no list gets standard speeds.
- Pocket 3, Pocket 4, Pocket 4 Pro, Nano and Action 6 offer the Auto ISO ceiling, the ISO limit and the native ISO hop again.
- The app no longer hangs while readouts update in a long session, and a volume-button shutter no longer causes a rare quit.
- Playback keeps Pause through buffering, seeking, Share and returning to the app.
- Playback stays smoother on large cards, scopes update more evenly, and wide clips keep correct brightness through LUTs and scopes.
- Selection, deletion, caching and sharing stay tied to the current clips, and sharing several cached clips works together.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip keeps downloading after you close it and reads CACHED.
- Sharing a clip you left mid-download in the player, or whose download stopped, finishes its copy instead of failing.
- VoiceOver skips live controls under a covering screen and the hidden buttons of a collapsed landscape palette.

### iOS: what to test

- On a full card, scroll the grid, select, use Select all, then Exit selection. Play a clip, pinch to zoom and pan to the screen edges.
- Reinstall and pair: Local Network comes before the Wi-Fi Join alert. Disconnect and connect again: live view starts without a freeze.
- During Live View, go Home for over 3 minutes, then stay in Media for 30 minutes: the picture returns each time without Retry.
- At 180 degrees/25p in Auto, check native ISO and Auto ISO limits. Select Manual, then 50p: 180 stays as 1/50 becomes 1/100.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip briefly, go back and Share it: the speed shows, it shares and reads CACHED.

## Android

### Android: new and changed

- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media controls look and behave like the other OpenCine apps.
- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting, Select all and transfer progress stay responsive while clips cache.
- Media grid is staggered: portrait photos stand upright and every item fills the space above it.
- Caching and downloads show a live speed beside the percentage, for example 18% and 42 MB/s.
- Selecting clips shows an Exit selection close beside the count, then All, Share and Delete. Media's top corner is now a Back arrow.
- Pinch-zoomed playback now fills the screen past the clip's letterbox, up to the screen edge.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting: the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tap the storage readout to step through free space, free percentage and remaining time, the same in portrait and landscape.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec, and in Photo mode white balance and focus move to the top corner so captions fit.
- Media scrolls to the newest clip. Offline, it says the camera is disconnected and shows the clips cached on this phone.
- The ISO drum stars the native ISO of the current colour profile, for example 400 in D-Log M.
- Bluetooth Remote Shutter is now off until you turn it on in Operator Setup > Controls. Your saved choice is kept.
- Diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate picture interruptions.

### Android: fixes

- The app no longer freezes or shows App not responding while the live picture rebuilds, for example after rotating or returning.
- Reconnecting waits in the background and resumes on return without Retry. The card names its 3-minute window; a dropped link shows no bars.
- Returning from Media, even after a long visit, restores the live picture instead of disconnecting the camera.
- Connection failures open the relevant Wi-Fi settings, and pairing timeouts explain what to try next.
- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual and frame-rate changes; the list is no longer stuck at 1/60, and a Pocket 3 with no list gets standard speeds.
- Pocket 3, Pocket 4, Pocket 4 Pro, Nano and Action 6 offer the Auto ISO ceiling, the ISO limit and the native ISO hop again.
- The live top bar readouts get their dark glow back, so they stay readable over bright pictures.
- Playback keeps Pause through buffering, scrubbing and Share.
- Playback stays smoother on large cards; scopes and audio meters update without redrawing the whole player.
- Clip deletion, caching and sharing several cached clips work together on the current selection. The selection bar shows the whole count.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip keeps downloading after you close it and reads CACHED. TalkBack reads it as one switch.
- Sharing a clip you left mid-download in the player, or whose download stopped, finishes its copy instead of failing.
- Shared clips keep their DJI file names instead of arriving with long random names.
- The open tools panel keeps the camera values visible beside it instead of hiding them. Portrait card-space labels keep their full value.

### Android: what to test

- On a full card, scroll the grid, select, use Select all, then Exit selection. Play a clip, pinch to zoom and pan to the screen edges.
- Rotate the phone repeatedly and leave and return to the app during Live View: the app stays responsive and the picture returns.
- In Live View, go Home, record 60 to 90 s with the system camera app, then return: the picture comes back by itself with no Retry.
- At 180 degrees/25p in Auto, check native ISO and Auto ISO limits. Select Manual, then 50p: 180 stays as 1/50 becomes 1/100.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip briefly, go back and Share it with a second clip: the speed shows and both keep their names.

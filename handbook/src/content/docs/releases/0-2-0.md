---
title: OpenPocketCine 0.2.0 release notes
description: iOS and Android changes for testers upgrading from 0.1.8.
---

0.2.0 starts a new version train after **0.1.8**. iOS and Android cover the same
release window; their store build numbers differ. It is a new version, so the first
external TestFlight build of 0.2.0 goes through TestFlight Beta App Review before
testers can install it.

Live streaming is new in this release and has not yet been proven against every
service on real hardware. Tell us what works and what does not in
[Discussions](https://github.com/erik-sutton95/OpenPocketCine/discussions).

## iOS

### iOS: new and changed

- Live stream the monitor to Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or a Custom server: add the service and stream key in Settings > Sharing > Live stream, then tap Go live.
- Go live sits under the timecode, top right. While live it reads LIVE with the elapsed time, and one line shows quality, bitrate and health. Stop asks once.
- Streaming works over a shared Wi-Fi network or the phone's hotspot. On the camera's own Wi-Fi, Go live is unavailable and says why.
- A Custom server can use plain rtmp:// (for TikTok LIVE); the card warns that the stream is unencrypted.
- When the camera feed is below 1080p, Quality offers 1080p upscaled beside Camera feed.
- Pocket 4 Pro: 1080p from camera. The camera sends its own 1080p stream to the phone over the hotspot or a saved shared network, and Go live relays it.
- If the monitor closes during a stream, or Multiview opens, a Live pill at the top keeps the stream visible with Stop. Leaving the app ends the stream.
- Hold zoom for the new zoom panel on the screen edge: current zoom, three presets, a ramp key and a vertical ruler. Drag the ruler for smooth zoom; the readout shows two decimals.
- Zoom presets: tap an empty slot, or hold any slot, to save the current zoom; tap a saved slot to go there. Preset ramp sets the move time, Off or up to 10 s.
- Pocket 4 Pro zooms to 18x on DJI's November 2026 firmware: double tap for 6x, 12x and 18x. Older firmware stops at 12x.
- Camera presets, first on the Settings Controls tab, save the camera's exposure and white balance under a name and recall them.
- GUIDES options add Action safe and Title safe, each with its own size, drawn inside the frame lines.
- The monitor's lock also holds the screen rotation, upside-down landscape included, so a phone mounted upside down no longer flips when the rig is levelled.

### iOS: fixes

- AirPods head tracking no longer moves the gimbal in FPV or Direction Lock; Calibrate asks for Follow or Tilt locked instead.
- When the gimbal is past the head tracking range, head tracking stops once and says so instead of freezing silently. The joystick still works.
- With subject tracking on, a frozen picture starts its first repair after about 2 s instead of about 6 s.
- A Pocket 4 Pro that reports an unfamiliar Wi-Fi mode joins a Wi-Fi or Hotspot setup and Multiview instead of being refused.

### iOS: what to test

- Save a stream key, connect over the phone's hotspot and tap Go live. Check the picture on the service, then Stop.
- Pocket 4 Pro: choose 1080p from camera and Go live over the hotspot, then over home Wi-Fi. Stop, dismiss the end notice and check the camera connects again.
- Hold zoom, drag the ruler, save three presets, set a Preset ramp of 2 s and tap between them.
- Save a Camera preset, change ISO, shutter and white balance, then recall it.
- Mount the phone upside down in landscape, tap the lock and level the rig: the monitor stays put.

## Android

### Android: new and changed

- Live stream the monitor to Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or a Custom server: add the service and stream key in Settings > Sharing > Live stream, then tap Go live.
- Go live sits under the timecode, top right. While live it reads LIVE with the elapsed time, and one line shows quality, bitrate and health. Stop asks once.
- Streaming works over a shared Wi-Fi network or the phone's hotspot. On the camera's own Wi-Fi, Go live is unavailable and says why.
- A Custom server can use plain rtmp:// (for TikTok LIVE); the card warns that the stream is unencrypted.
- When the camera feed is below 1080p, Quality offers 1080p upscaled beside Camera feed.
- If the monitor closes during a stream, or Multiview opens, a Live pill at the top keeps the stream visible with Stop. Leaving the app ends the stream, with Restart on return.
- Hold zoom for the new zoom panel on the screen edge: current zoom, three presets, a ramp key and a vertical ruler. Drag the ruler for smooth zoom; the readout shows two decimals.
- Zoom presets: tap an empty slot, or hold any slot, to save the current zoom; tap a saved slot to go there. Preset ramp sets the move time, Off or up to 10 s.
- Pocket 4 Pro zooms to 18x on DJI's November 2026 firmware: double tap for 6x, 12x and 18x. Older firmware stops at 12x.
- Camera presets, first on the Settings Controls tab, save the camera's exposure and white balance under a name and recall them.
- GUIDES options add Action safe and Title safe, each with its own size, drawn inside the frame lines.
- The photo viewer has the same View Assist bar as clips, with peaking, false colour and zebra.
- Multiview matches iPhone: toolbar order Layout, Camera, Fit, LUT; tiles show REC with the time or STBY, and the format line reads like 4K 25p · D-Log M.
- The Multiview settings popup follows iPhone's order and stays open across a record start or stop.
- The monitor's lock also holds the screen rotation, so a phone mounted upside down no longer turns when the rig is levelled.

### Android: fixes

- A Pocket 4 Pro that reports an unfamiliar Wi-Fi mode joins a Wi-Fi or Hotspot setup and Multiview instead of being refused.
- A rejected or unconfirmed record in Multiview shows its note on the tile.

### Android: what to test

- Save a stream key, connect over the phone's hotspot and tap Go live. Check the picture on the service, then Stop.
- Hold zoom, drag the ruler, save three presets, set a Preset ramp of 2 s and tap between them.
- Save a Camera preset, change ISO, shutter and white balance, then recall it.
- In Multiview, start and stop recording with the settings popup open: the popup stays and the tiles show REC and STBY.
- Lock the monitor in reverse landscape and turn the phone: the monitor stays put until you unlock.
